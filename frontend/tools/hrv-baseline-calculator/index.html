HRV Baseline Calculator

Compare your latest morning rMSSD reading with your own recent baseline. Paste a run of nightly readings and choose a 7, 14 or 28-night window to get the mean, the sample standard deviation, the coefficient of variation, the latest reading's z-score, the trend, a band chart and a reading-by-reading table. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

Nightly readings

Separate values with commas, spaces, semicolons or new lines. Keep the most recent reading last.

A weekly window reacts quickly; 28 nights is steadier.

    What this does not do

    • It reports descriptive statistics for the numbers you paste. It does not diagnose anything, score training readiness or tell you what to do.
    • rMSSD values from different devices, sensors, body positions or measurement times are not directly comparable, and a single morning reading carries its own measurement noise.
    • Nothing is saved: reloading the page clears your readings.
