Enter a step count and your step length to see the distance.

One step is a single footfall; a stride is two steps. Step length varies with height, pace, terrain and footwear, so the 76 cm default is only a starting point — measure your own for a useful answer. The conversion is exact arithmetic on the length you enter; a GPS watch or pedometer will still differ because it samples distance and counts steps separately. Walking estimates only, not a fitness or medical measurement. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
