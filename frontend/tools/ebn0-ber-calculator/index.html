For coherent detection over an additive white Gaussian noise channel the bit error rate is a Gaussian tail. BPSK and QPSK give Q(sqrt(2·Eb/N0)), 8-PSK gives (2/3)·Q(sqrt(6·Eb/N0)·sin(π/8)), and square M-QAM gives (4/k)(1 − 1/sqrt(M))·Q(sqrt(3k·Eb/N0/(M−1))) with k = log2M bits per symbol. Those are the standard Gray-coded approximations, close to the exact curves above about 1e-3. The required Eb/N0 for a target BER is found by bisection on the same curve, and the margin is your Eb/N0 minus that requirement. Es/N0 is Eb/N0 plus 10·log10(k) when the noise bandwidth equals the symbol rate. The model is uncoded and assumes perfect carrier and timing recovery, a flat channel and no interference, fading, phase noise or coding gain, so treat it as a planning and teaching estimate rather than a link certification.
