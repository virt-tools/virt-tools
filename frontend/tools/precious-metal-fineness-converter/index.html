Precious Metal Fineness Converter

Convert a gold, silver or platinum purity between karat, parts per thousand and percent, and see the hallmark mark it sits on.

Karat is the gold content in parts per 24 by mass, so 14 karat is 583.3‰; millesimal fineness is parts per 1,000, which is what a hallmark shows, and 14 karat is stamped 585. Silver and platinum are marked in thousandths and have no karat figure. The page converts a declared stamp; it does not assay, test or value the metal. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
