Medication Refill Runway

Plan refill timing from medication-label instructions and your own inventory records.

Follow the prescription label and contact your pharmacist or clinician about any discrepancy, shortage, authorization question, missed/extra dose, or travel need. This calculator only organizes values you enter; it does not give medical advice or recommend rationing, skipping, splitting, doubling, or any other dosing change. It does not assess emergencies.

Privacy: Calculations stay in this page. Nothing is uploaded. Data is kept in memory unless you explicitly save it in this browser; downloads, printouts, and browser saves may expose sensitive health information on a shared device.
