Hearing Exposure Calculator

Screen combined daily A-weighted noise segments under NIOSH or OSHA dose assumptions.

Occupational screening only—not medical advice or a compliance determination. Real decisions require calibrated measurements, correct instrument settings, task coverage, and qualified safety review. This simple model does not evaluate impulse/impact peaks, frequency content, hearing-protector fit, protection variability, ototoxic exposures, or individual susceptibility. Do not subtract a labeled protector rating from measurements without an appropriate assessment. Sudden hearing loss, severe ear symptoms, or symptoms after a blast warrant prompt medical evaluation; use emergency services for an immediate emergency.

Exposure segments

Use A-weighted equivalent/average levels for continuous segments. Up to 200 rows, 40–140 dBA, 0.001–1,440 minutes each, and no more than 1,440 entered minutes total.

Method and threshold

Segments below the selected instrument threshold contribute 0% to the displayed standard dose and TWA. They remain in the energy Leq calculation and audit table. This threshold behavior can materially change results near the cutoff.
