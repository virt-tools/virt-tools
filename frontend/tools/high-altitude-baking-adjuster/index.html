High-Altitude Baking Adjuster

Scale a baking recipe for elevation. Tell it how high you are baking and what the recipe calls for, and it returns the reduced leavening, sugar and flour, the extra liquid, and the hotter oven the published high-altitude charts call for.

The figures use the middle of each published range, interpolated between the 3,000, 5,000 and 7,000 ft bands; above 7,000 ft the top band is held and below 3,000 ft nothing changes. Adjust one ingredient at a time and try the smaller change first. A hotter oven sets the structure sooner, so start checking a few minutes before the recipe's usual time. Amounts are US cups and tablespoons (1 cup = 16 tbsp). Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.
