World Athletics Technical Rules set the shot put, discus and hammer landing sector at 34.92° and the javelin sector at 28.96°, between the inner edges of two lines that, if extended, pass through the centre of the circle or, for the javelin, through the centre of the foul arc 8 m behind it. Because twice the sine of half of 34.92° is 0.6001 and twice the sine of half of 28.96° is 0.5001, the straight distance between the ends of two equal tape runs is 0.60 times their length for the circle events and 0.50 times for the javelin, which is the rule-book shortcut of 12 m across at 20 m, 48 m at 80 m and 60 m at 100 m. The arc at that distance is the angle in radians times the radius and the landing area inside it is half the angle in radians times the radius squared. A 0.1° sector that is too wide moves the cross distance by the amount shown, and USATF rule 187.22 permits 34.92° ± 0.1°. Inside diameters are 2.135 m for the shot and hammer circles and 2.50 m for the discus circle; the javelin runway is 4 m wide and its foul arc has an 8 m radius, so a throw measured from the arc lands that much further from the arc centre. This is marking arithmetic only: it assumes level ground and a sector centred on the throwing direction, it does not survey a site or replace the competition's own field regulations, and measuring still needs a tape, pegs and a second pair of hands.
