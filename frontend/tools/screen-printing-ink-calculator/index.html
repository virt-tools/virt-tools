The pitch is the mesh count converted to threads per inch, 25400 ÷ count in micrometres; the opening is the pitch minus the thread diameter; the open area is (opening ÷ pitch)²; and the theoretical ink volume is the open fraction × fabric thickness, in cm³ per m², which is the same number as the wet film thickness in micrometres. One open fraction of a square metre filled to that depth is one cubic centimetre, so area × volume gives the ink per print in millilitres. This is the geometric ceiling for a new, flat, tensioned fabric: the stencil, ink rheology, squeegee angle and pressure, off-contact, tension and the substrate all reduce the ink actually transferred, so treat the figure as a comparison between meshes and an order-of-magnitude quantity, not a promise of the printed film.
