Four-Part Writing Checker

Check an SATB progression for voices out of range, crossings, wide spacing, voice overlap and parallel perfect fifths or octaves.

One chord per line, four notes in the order soprano, alto, tenor, bass: G4 E4 C4 C3. A note is a letter A–G, an optional # or b, and an octave number. Up to eight chords; bar lines are ignored.

The check covers the mechanical part-writing rules only: the usual choral ranges (soprano C4–C6, alto G3–E5, tenor C3–G4, bass E2–C4), voice crossing, an octave limit between neighbouring upper voices, voice overlap, and parallel perfect fifths and octaves between consecutive chords. It does not judge harmony, chord choice, doubling, dissonance treatment, spacing from tenor to bass, or style, and ranges vary between choirs and editions. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
