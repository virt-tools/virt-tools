Time Scale Converter

Turn a UTC date and time into TAI, GPS time, GPS week and seconds of week, and TT, using the IERS leap-second table.

UTC keeps within 0.9 s of the Earth's rotation by inserting leap seconds; TAI runs on atomic clocks and never stops for one, so TAI sits 37 s ahead of UTC today. GPS time is TAI minus 19 s and also ignores leap seconds, so it runs 18 s ahead of UTC: week 0 began 1980-01-06 and the week counter rolls over every 1,024 weeks. TT (terrestrial time) is TAI plus 32.184 s. The table holds the 27 leap seconds announced between 1972 and 2016, so dates after 2016-12-31 assume no further leap second. Enter 23:59:60 on a leap-second day to convert that inserted second itself. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
