Beat Cut Planner

Enter a tempo, a meter and a project frame rate to get a frame-accurate cut list: the length of a beat and a bar, the frames in each, the SMPTE timecode and frame number of every cut, and how far a fixed whole-frame spacing would drift. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    What this does not do

    • This page does not detect beats. Enter the tempo your DAW, the track metadata or a tap-tempo reading gives; a tempo that is 0.5% out has moved a beat by half a frame after about a hundred beats.
    • Live performance drifts, fades and edits that stretch or shorten clips all move the real downbeat away from a click, so check the cut list against the waveform before committing.
    • Timecode is counted at the nominal rate: drop-frame labels at 29.97 and 59.94 skip frame numbers to track the clock, while 23.976 uses the standard non-drop label, which runs about 0.1% behind wall-clock time.
    • Nothing is saved: reloading the page clears your entries.
