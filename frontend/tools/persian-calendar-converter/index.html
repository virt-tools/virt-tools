Persian Calendar Converter

Turn a Gregorian date into its Solar Hijri (Persian) date — or a Persian date back into the civil calendar — with the weekday, month length and day of the Persian year.

Direction

The Solar Hijri year starts at Nowruz, so 1 Farvardin falls on 20 or 21 March. Gregorian years 1800 to 2100 are supported.

This page uses the arithmetic Solar Hijri calendar: a 33-year cycle whose leap years are those where (25 × year + 11) mod 33 is less than 8, which puts Nowruz on 20 or 21 March and gives Esfand 29 days in a common year and 30 in a leap year. Iran fixes Nowruz astronomically, from the March equinox measured at Tehran, so a year whose equinox falls close to noon can start a day earlier or later than the arithmetic rule; over the supported range the two agree with the published Nowruz dates, but treat this as a calendar converter rather than an official announcement. A Persian day begins at midnight, unlike the lunar Hijri day, so the civil weekday and the Persian weekday always match. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
