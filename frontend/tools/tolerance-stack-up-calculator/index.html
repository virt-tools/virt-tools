What the two numbers mean

The worst-case total is the arithmetic sum of the tolerance magnitudes: it is the largest the chain can become if every dimension lands at its limit in the same direction. It never fails, but it assumes the least likely outcome of every part at once.

The RSS total is the root-sum-square of the same magnitudes, √(t₁² + t₂² + … + tₙ²). It assumes the errors are independent, centred on nominal and roughly normal, so a chain of many dimensions rarely reaches its arithmetic extreme. RSS needs a real process behind it: an inspected sample, a capable supplier, or a control plan. With few dimensions, or with tolerances that drift together, the worst case is the honest answer.

Signs are already inside the nominal total, so a subtracting dimension lowers it; its tolerance still widens the range by its own magnitude in both directions.
