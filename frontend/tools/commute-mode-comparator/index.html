Commute Mode Comparator

Compare user-entered commute modes by distance, time, per-person cost, emissions, and optional activity estimates.

Your assumptions drive every result. Example presets are illustrative, editable placeholders—not authoritative or current prices, emissions factors, calorie estimates, route times, or recommendations. No routes, schedules, traffic, fares, fuel prices, or external data are fetched.

Comparison settings

Fixed and distance-based cost plus emissions are entered at vehicle/group level and divided by occupancy. Use occupancy 1 when a factor is already per-person. Calories are entered per person-km and are never occupancy-adjusted.
