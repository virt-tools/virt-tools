Rep Tempo Calculator

Turn a four-phase lifting tempo into seconds per rep, time under tension per set and the clock time of the whole block, rests included.

Tempo is written the usual four-number way — seconds lowering, seconds paused at the bottom, seconds lifting, seconds paused at the top — so 3-1-2-0 is a three-second lower, a one-second pause, a two-second lift and no top pause. Seconds per rep is the sum of the four phases, and time under tension for a set is that per-rep time multiplied by the reps; multiplying again by the sets gives the block total. Clock time adds the rests between sets, and the work-to-rest ratio compares one set's tension time with one rest. The tool is timing arithmetic on your own inputs: it does not judge whether a tempo, rep range or rest is appropriate for you, and it is not coaching or medical advice. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
