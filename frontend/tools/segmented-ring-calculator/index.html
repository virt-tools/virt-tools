Each segment is a trapezoid cut from a strip whose width is the ring wall, with both ends mitered at 180° ÷ segments from square. The outer edge is the chord D × sin(180° ÷ segments) of the finished diameter, the inner edge is the same chord on the inner diameter, and the cut length adds one kerf so the saw removes it. A ring of n segments has n glue joints and needs n cut lengths of strip. Segment lengths are measured along the outer edge and rounded to 0.001 mm. Miter joints in segmented work are usually expected to close within about a tenth of a degree, so set the saw, cut a test ring in scrap and check the fit before cutting the project stock. Glue-line thickness, blade runout, snipe, ring stacking and the hollowing allowance are not modelled, and the result is a cut list rather than a structural claim.
