Social Jetlag Calculator

Compare the sleep window you keep on workdays with the one you keep on free days. The tool finds the midpoint of each window and reports how far it moves, which is the quantity chronobiologists call social jetlag. It also shows both sleep durations and how much longer free-day sleep is, so you can see whether the shift is mostly timing or partly recovery sleep. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    What this does not do

    • It is time arithmetic on four clock times you type. It does not measure sleep, diagnose a sleep disorder or give medical advice; persistent insomnia, excessive sleepiness or breathing pauses during sleep deserve qualified medical advice.
    • A single pair of nights ignores naps, shift rotation, weekends actually worked, travel and seasonal daylight. A free-day window that is much longer than the workday window mixes a later body clock with recovery sleep.
    • Mid-sleep times and the shift are shown to the nearest minute; the arithmetic itself keeps the exact half-minutes that an odd-length sleep window produces.
    • Nothing is saved: reloading the page clears the form and the result.
