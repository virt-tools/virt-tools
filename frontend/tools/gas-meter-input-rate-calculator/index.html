Gas Meter Input Rate Calculator

A gas appliance's rating plate says how much fuel it should burn. Clocking the meter says how much it really burns: time one revolution of the small test dial, and this page turns that into cubic feet per hour, BTU per hour and kilowatts, then compares the result with the nameplate input.

The result is a measured fuel input: it does not tell you that combustion is safe or that the appliance is efficient. The page makes no network requests and stores nothing.

How the calculation works

The test dial measures volume, so the flow follows from how long one revolution takes, and the energy follows from the heating value of the fuel your utility delivers.

  • Flow. ft³/h = dial size × revolutions × 3,600 ÷ seconds. A ½ ft³ dial that turns once in 20 seconds means 90 ft³ per hour.
  • Input. BTU/h = ft³/h × heating value. At 1,030 BTU/ft³ the same 90 ft³/h is 92,700 BTU/h. Kilowatts are BTU/h ÷ 3,412.14 and therms are BTU/h ÷ 100,000.
  • Comparison. percent of nameplate = input ÷ nameplate input × 100. ANSI/ACCA 5 QI asks for a gas firing rate within ±5% of the nameplate input, and lists clocking the meter as one of the accepted procedures; the page flags anything outside that band and anything outside ±10%.
  • Timing sensitivity. Because the flow varies as 1/time, a one-second error changes the answer by roughly the measured input divided by the seconds timed. Time three revolutions and divide when the dial turns quickly.
  • Reading it. An input above the plate usually means over-firing from high manifold pressure or the wrong orifices; below it means under-firing, a derated orifice for altitude, or a heating value lower than the default. Only clock the stage you are checking on a two-stage or modulating appliance.

This page is arithmetic for a field measurement, not a combustion analysis. It does not measure manifold pressure, temperature rise, CO or efficiency, and it cannot confirm that an appliance is burning safely. Imperial ft³ test dials only: metric meters are read on the drum over a timed interval and digital meters without a test dial cannot be clocked this way. Nothing is uploaded, and the page works offline.
