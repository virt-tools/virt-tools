Laminated Dough Layer Calculator

Turn a fold plan into a layer count, check the butter ratio, and see how thick each layer is once the sheet is rolled to its final thickness.

Folds are counted from the block you start with, butter block enclosed in the dough. A letter fold brings the two outer thirds over the middle; a book fold brings both outer edges to the centre.

Each letter fold multiplies the sheets crossing a cut by three (3 × 3 × 3 = 27 layers after three of them), each book fold multiplies them by four, and a mixed plan multiplies both factors together. The block before any fold counts as one layer. The average layer is the final sheet thickness divided by that count; the count and the ratio assume the butter stays in one unbroken sheet and every fold spans the full width. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.
