Staged checklist

    Assumptions: An authoritative TTL change is immediately available and a resolver that queries just before an event may retain its answer for the entire applicable TTL. The conservative pre-lowering cohort expires at “TTL lowered + current TTL”; a cache refreshed just before cutover expires at “record change + reduced TTL.” The optional negative cohort uses the supplied effective negative TTL. Resolver TTL floors/caps, prefetch, forwarding layers, DNS provider delays, clock skew, serve-stale behavior, record-specific behavior, failures, and client/application caches are not modeled. DNS propagation cannot be guaranteed from TTL arithmetic alone.
