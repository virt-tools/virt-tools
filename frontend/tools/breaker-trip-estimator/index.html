Enter breaker rating and overload current.

Thermal trip times follow a generic I²t curve anchored to the IEC 60898 conventional limits (no trip within 1 h at 1.13× rating; trip within 1 h at 1.45×). Real trip curves vary by manufacturer and trip-curve type (B/C/D), and magnetic trip thresholds differ. All calculations run locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device.
