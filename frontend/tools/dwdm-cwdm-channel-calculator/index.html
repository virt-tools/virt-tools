DWDM & CWDM Channel Calculator

193.10 THz is ITU channel n = 0 at 1552.52 nm. Enter a laser frequency or wavelength and pick the grid to get the channel index, the exact grid frequency and wavelength, the band and how far the reading sits from the channel.

The grid anchors the dense plan at 193.10 THz (1552.52 nm); the 50 GHz plan adds the half channels. Coarse channels sit every 20 nm from 1271 to 1611 nm. A reading between channels snaps to the nearest one and the offset is shown. A unit typed after the number overrides the selector.

The DWDM grid is defined in frequency, not wavelength: every channel is 193.10 THz plus a whole number of 100 or 50 GHz steps, so the 100 GHz channels are about 0.8 nm apart around 1550 nm. Wavelengths here are vacuum values, which is how the ITU grid is written; light inside a fiber travels slower and its wavelength is shorter by the fiber's index. Band edges follow the ITU letters: O 1260–1360, E 1360–1460, S 1460–1530, C 1530–1565, L 1565–1625 and U 1625–1675 nm. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
