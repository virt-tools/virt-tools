Total line to paint—

Law 1 fixes the goal at 7.32 m between the inside of the posts and 2.44 m high, the goal-area lines 5.5 m from the inside of each post and 5.5 m into the field, and the penalty-area lines 16.5 m from the inside of each post and 16.5 m into the field. Those distances make the goal area 18.32 m wide and the penalty area 40.32 m wide. The penalty mark sits 11 m from the goal line, so the arc of radius 9.15 m about it leaves the area 5.5 m from the mark and is drawn as a 16.94 m arc outside the area; the centre circle is the full 9.15 m circle, the halfway line joins the touchlines and each corner is a 1 m quarter circle. The total is the sum of those runs, paint area is that length times the line width, and paint per coat is the area divided by the coverage printed on the tin, so it covers one coat on a sound surface. Marks and goals are listed but not counted as line. This is marking arithmetic only: it does not survey a site, set competition markings or replace the competition's own field regulations, and it covers 11-a-side pitches rather than small-sided formats.
