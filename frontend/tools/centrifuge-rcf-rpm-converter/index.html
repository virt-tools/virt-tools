Centrifuge RCF & RPM Converter

Turn a centrifuge speed into relative centrifugal force, or a force back into the speed a rotor needs, from the radius of the rotor.

Direction

Relative centrifugal force is the acceleration at a point in the rotor divided by standard gravity, RCF = ω²r/g with ω = 2πn/60, so at a radius r in centimetres it reduces to RCF = 1.11808 × 10⁻⁵ × r × n². The force changes along the tube: the radius runs from the spindle axis to the bottom of the tube for rmax, or to the middle of the liquid column for rav, and a rotor's manual says which one its table uses. A protocol written in × g transfers to another rotor unchanged; the speed that produces it does not, which is why this page needs the radius. Radii are limited to 1–1,000 mm, speeds to 200,000 rpm and forces to 5,000,000 × g. Check the rotor's rated speed and the tube's fill limit — nothing here is a centrifugation protocol.
