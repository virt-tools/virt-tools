Solar Time Calculator

Estimate solar noon, sunrise, and sunset for a date, latitude, and longitude using the Cooper declination and a simple equation-of-time model. Results are in the standard time at the UTC offset you enter (no DST adjustment). Runs locally in your browser.

Approximate model: the declination uses Cooper's formula and the equation of time uses the standard three-term approximation, so sunrise/sunset can differ from almanac values by a few minutes. Sunrise and sunset assume a flat horizon at sea level and include standard atmospheric refraction (sun center at −0.833°). Extreme latitudes can return polar day or polar night.
