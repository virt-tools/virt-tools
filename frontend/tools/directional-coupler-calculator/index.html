Enter the four port powers to see the coupler parameters.

Coupling is 10 log(P1/P3), directivity is 10 log(P3/P4), isolation is their sum, and insertion loss is 10 log(P1/P2). Directivity assumes the through port is terminated in a matched load, so the isolated-port reading is leakage through the coupler rather than a reflection. The port powers should add up to no more than the input of a passive coupler; the page flags a total above 101%. Everything is calculated in this page: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
