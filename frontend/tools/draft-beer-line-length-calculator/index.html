Draft Beer Line Length Calculator

Balance a keg system: 12 psi with the tap 2 ft above the keg centre needs 3 ft 8 in of 3/16 in vinyl line. Enter the serving pressure, the lift and any tower or fitting resistance, then read the line length that pours at about 2 fl oz per second.

The regulator pressure is the gas pressure you pour at. Height is measured from the middle of the keg to the faucet; enter a negative number when the tap sits below the keg. A tower usually adds 0 to 8 psi, and only its maker knows the figure.

Balance means the resistance of the system cancels the pressure pushing the beer, so it arrives at the faucet at about atmospheric pressure and pours roughly 2 fl oz per second — an eight-second pint. The lift costs about 0.5 psi per foot, and the line supplies the rest at the resistance of its bore. Table values are nominal: tubing brand, temperature, fittings and tower move the real figure, so cut the line a little long and trim an inch at a time until a pint fills in about eight seconds.
