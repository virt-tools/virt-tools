The area under the single-sideband curve is integrated over the band you choose: σφ = √(2 · ∫10L(f)/10 df) rad, doubled for the two sidebands, then divided by 2πf0 to reach seconds. Only offsets inside the band count, so the band start is what separates wander from jitter. Spurs, residual FM, period jitter, amplitude noise and anything outside the band are not modelled, and nothing leaves this page.
