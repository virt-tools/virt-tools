Fermentation Salt Calculator

Calculate salt with the percentage denominator stated explicitly and preserve exact quantities alongside practical rounding.

Salt arithmetic does not establish food safety. Use a current, scientifically tested recipe for the specific food and process; do not reduce its required salt, acid, food, or water proportions. Control the specified fermentation temperature and time, use clean equipment and sound produce, keep food submerged when the recipe requires it, monitor acidity exactly as the validated process directs, and discard food showing spoilage rather than tasting it. People at increased risk from foodborne illness should seek guidance from a qualified health professional and food-preservation authority.
