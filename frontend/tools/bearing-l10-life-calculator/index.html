Enter the rating, load and speed to calculate the bearing life.

The basic rating life is the life reached or exceeded by 90 % of a large group of apparently identical bearings before the first signs of fatigue: L10 = (C/P)p in millions of revolutions, and L10h = (106 / (60 n)) · (C/P)p hours, with p = 3 for ball bearings and p = 10/3 for roller bearings. It assumes a constant load and speed, good-quality steel and conventional lubrication, and it says nothing about static load rating, minimum load or a modified life with contamination and lubrication factors. This is a preliminary engineering estimate, not a design review or a guarantee of service life. Everything runs locally in your browser.
