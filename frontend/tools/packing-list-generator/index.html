Packing List Generator

Turn a trip into a grouped packing list. Choose the length, climate, laundry access and activities; the page works out how many of each item to take, and you can check things off or print the list. Everything runs locally in your browser.

Up to 12 items, 60 characters each.
Set your trip details, then build the list.

Quantities are practical starting points, not a guarantee: add medication, adapters, or anything the trip specifically needs. Laundry halves the base clothing count and adds one drying day. The list is generated in the page and is never stored, uploaded or sent; printing uses your browser's print dialog.
