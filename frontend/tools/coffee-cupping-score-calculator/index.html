Coffee Cupping Score Calculator

Add up a cupping form on the 6–10 scale and see the total out of 100 and its grade band.

Cup attributes

Score each attribute from 6.00 to 10.00 in steps of 0.25, where 6 is good and 10 is exceptional.

All seven scores use the same 6–10 scale and quarter-point steps. Decimal commas are accepted.

Cup consistency

Each of the five cups earns 2 points per row when it shows no fault, so a clean flight scores 5 cups in each row.

Count the cups, out of five, that were uniform, clean and sweet. Each counted cup is worth 2 points in that row.

Defects

A tainted cup costs 2 points and a faulty cup costs 4. The two counts cannot add up to more than the five cups.

The form follows the specialty-coffee cupping layout: six descriptive attributes and the overall score are marked on the same 6–10 scale in quarter-point steps, while uniformity, clean cup and sweetness award 2 points for each of the five cups and defects deduct 2 points per tainted cup and 4 per faulty cup. The total is exactly the sum of the scores you enter, so it is only as good as those scores: this page does not taste anything, does not replace a calibrated cupper or a second opinion, and makes no claim about a coffee's commercial grade. A total of 80 or more is the usual specialty threshold. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
