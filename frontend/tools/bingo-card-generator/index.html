Bingo Card Generator

Turn your own word or phrase list into printable bingo cards. Every card gets its own layout, the centre can be free, and a shuffled call list is ready to read out. Runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

One entry per line. Short words, names or numbers read best — long phrases wrap inside the square.

Enter at least enough words to fill one card, then generate.

Each card draws a different random selection and arrangement from your list, so two cards rarely match. A 4 × 4 grid has no single centre square and cannot use a free space. Words are used as written — no spell-check or translation — and duplicate entries are ignored. Printing uses your browser's print dialog; choose portrait, and disable headers and footers for the cleanest sheets.
