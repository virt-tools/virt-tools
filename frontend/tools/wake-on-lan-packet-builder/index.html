Wake-on-LAN Packet Builder

Turn a network card's MAC address into the exact magic packet that wakes it, with or without a SecureOn password.

the sleeping card's own unicast address

Enter a MAC address to build its magic packet.

A magic packet is six ff bytes followed by the target MAC repeated sixteen times — 102 bytes, or 108 with a six-byte SecureOn password on the end. It is sent as a UDP datagram to the limited broadcast address 255.255.255.255 on port 9 (some equipment listens on port 7). Browsers cannot send raw UDP, so copy this hex into a wake tool, a switch or router wake action, or a script; the card only answers on the same Layer 2 segment, and Wake-on-LAN must be enabled in the firmware with standby power present. Separators in the MAC are optional, and the dotted Cisco form is accepted. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
