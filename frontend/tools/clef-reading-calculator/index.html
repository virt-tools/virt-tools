A clef fixes one written letter to one line: the G clef marks G4 on its line, the F clef marks F3, and the C clef marks C4. Positions run from three ledger lines below the staff to three ledger lines above it. An accidental changes the pitch of that line or space but never moves the note, and a clef with an octave change sounds an octave lower than written, so a written C4 in the tenor-voice treble clef sounds C3. Middle C is the first ledger line below the treble staff, line 3 in alto clef, line 4 in tenor clef and the first ledger line above the bass staff.
