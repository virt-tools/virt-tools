Model and scope: Presets are convenient mathematical starting points, not authoritative recipes or quality claims. The ratio is coffee mass : contact-water mass, using 1 mL water ≈ 1 g for planning. Expected beverage = contact water − (coffee grams × retained mL/g) + bypass water. Bypass is not included in the brew ratio or staged pours. Grind, temperature, agitation, pressure, extraction, dissolved coffee solids, evaporation, equipment retention, water composition, taste, and measurement error are not modeled. Stage percentages and timer examples are editable planning values, not brewing requirements.
