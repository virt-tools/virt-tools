GAA is 60 × goals against ÷ minutes played — the goals a goaltender allows per 60 minutes. Shots on goal include the goals that went in, so shots faced is saves plus goals against, and save percentage is saves ÷ shots faced. A goalie who faced no shots has no save percentage, shown as a dash. Enter only goals charged to the goaltender: official GAA leaves out empty-net and shootout goals, and overtime goals and time are included.
