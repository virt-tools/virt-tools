Lost-Wax Casting Metal Weight Calculator

Turn a weighed wax pattern into the metal to melt: the model's volume from the wax density, the metal it takes from that volume, and a button and sprue allowance on top.

Injection and carving waxes run about 0.95 g/cm³; the field method that treats the wax weight as cubic centimetres uses 1.00 instead, then adds the button.

The metal fills the volume the wax occupied, so metal weight = wax weight ÷ wax specific gravity × metal specific gravity, and the allowance adds the sprue and button metal that must be in the crucible: total = model metal × (1 + allowance ÷ 100). Metal densities are the specific gravities published by Rio Grande for casting alloys, and the model volume is the wax weight divided by the wax density. The metal on hand is optional: it shows the surplus or shortfall against the melt and the largest wax pattern that amount can cast at the same allowance. Alloy figures are typical, not a supplier certificate, and the model assumes solid wax with no internal voids, a known pattern weight with sprues, and a button size matched to your flask and sprue design; it does not cover shrinkage, flask temperature or the pour itself, and it does not authorise melting or casting metal. Everything is computed in this browser: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
