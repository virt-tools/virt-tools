The estimate assumes steady traffic and one data record per observed flow when it expires, so the record rate equals the rate at which new flows enter the cache. A 1-in-N interval is treated as independent per-packet selection: a flow with k packets is exported with probability 1 - (1 - 1/N)^k, which is why short flows are under-represented. Templates, option records, retransmissions and transport overhead are not added, and an inactive timeout can flush entries earlier than the cache figure assumes. Volume uses decimal GB (10^9 bytes). This page is arithmetic only: it does not read, query or configure any exporter or collector.
