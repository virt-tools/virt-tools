Household Water Audit

Model water activities from explicit assumptions and measurements without uploading household data.

Modeling limits: this tool does not infer leaks, bills, conservation mandates, product performance, or universal “normal” usage. Results reflect only entered activities, frequencies, units, and seasonal months. Active months uniformly scale annual totals; daily and monthly outputs are annual averages, not a month-by-month calendar. Zero, large, and household-basis flags are prompts to review inputs—not conclusions.
