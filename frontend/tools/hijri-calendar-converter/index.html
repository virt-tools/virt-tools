Hijri Calendar Converter

Turn a Gregorian date into its Hijri date — or a Hijri date back into the civil calendar — with the weekday, month length and day of the Hijri year.

Direction

The Hijri year is about eleven days shorter than the solar year, so the months move through the seasons. Gregorian years 1600 to 2200 are supported.

This is the tabular (arithmetic) Islamic calendar, the civil calendar used for history and for date arithmetic: a 30-year cycle with eleven leap years, odd months of 30 days, even months of 29, and Dhu al-Hijjah extended to 30 days in a leap year. It is not an observation-based calendar, so a date here can fall a day or two away from the Umm al-Qura calendar or from a locally sighted month; for Ramadan, Eid and other religious dates follow your community's announced calendar. A Hijri day begins at sunset, so the evening before a civil date already belongs to the next Hijri day; no time of day or location is taken. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
