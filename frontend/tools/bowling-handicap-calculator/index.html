A handicap levels a league by giving pins to the lower average. The usual rule is (base − average) × percentage ÷ 100, rounded to the nearest whole pin with halves rounded up and never below zero. A league picks its own base (often 200, 210 or 220) and percentage (often 80, 85 or 90), and the handicap is added to every game, so a 3-game series carries three times the per-game figure. Averages with a decimal are used exactly before the rounds. Negative (plus) handicaps for averages above the base, league maximums, average updates and tournament allowances are not modelled: check your league sheet for those, and everything is worked out in your browser with nothing stored or sent anywhere.
