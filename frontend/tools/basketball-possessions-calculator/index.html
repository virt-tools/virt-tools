Possession accounting

TermCountContribution

The estimate adds field goal attempts to 0.44 times free throw attempts, subtracts offensive rebounds (a put-back keeps the same possession alive) and adds turnovers. The 0.44 factor reflects that not every free throw attempt ends a possession, because the last of two or three is often made and team rebounds do not always follow; it is the standard box-score estimate, not an official count. Points per possession is points divided by estimated possessions, offensive rating is that figure per 100 possessions, pace rescales possessions to a 48-minute game, and turnover rate is turnovers divided by attempts plus turnovers. Nothing is stored, uploaded or sent anywhere.
