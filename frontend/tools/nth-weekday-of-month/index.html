The finder works entirely in the Gregorian calendar, so a year before 1583 is refused rather than converted. Weekdays are derived from an integer day count, not from a local Date object, so no time zone can shift the answer; the ISO 8601 week is the week containing the year's first Thursday, and the last occurrence is counted from the end of the month. A fifth occurrence exists only in months long enough to hold it, and the page says how many the month has when it does not. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
