USDA consumer weight classes are minimum net weights per dozen in the shell: Peewee 15 oz, Small 18 oz, Medium 21 oz, Large 24 oz, Extra large 27 oz, Jumbo 30 oz. Each egg here is taken at that per-dozen rate, so Large is 56.7 g, Medium 49.6 g and Extra large 63.8 g. Real eggs vary inside a carton and the legal class describes the dozen, not one egg, so weigh the cracked eggs when a recipe is tight. Weights include the shell. This converts quantity only — not volume, yolks or whites, and it does not grade or check eggs. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
