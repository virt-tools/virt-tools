Commissioning estimate—not a substitute for measurement or safe system setup. This model uses direct path distance only; it excludes loudspeaker/processor latency, phase and crossover behavior, driver offsets, reflections, wind, temperature gradients, audience geometry, and level differences. Delay cannot make an already-late acoustic path arrive earlier. Verify with suitable measurement tools and qualified system practice. Protect hearing and follow applicable sound-level limits during testing.
