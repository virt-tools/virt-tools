Worm Gear Calculator

Size a worm and wheel pair from module, starts and teeth: ratio, pitch diameters, centre distance, lead angle, sliding speed, efficiency and whether the drive is self-locking.

The lead angle is γ = atan(z1m / d1) and the wheel's pitch diameter is m·z2. Efficiency is the standard estimate η = (cos αn − μ tan γ) / (cos αn + μ cot γ), which depends on the friction coefficient you choose; it is not a gearbox rating. Check the manufacturer's power, thermal and lubrication data before building the drive. Everything is computed in this browser: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
