Roller Chain Drive Calculator

Size a two-sprocket roller chain drive: pitch diameters, ratio, the chain length the centres need, the even link count to buy, the centres it gives and the wrap on the small sprocket.

The pitch diameter is d = p / sin(π/z) and the chain length in pitches is L = 2C/p + (z1 + z2)/2 + ((z2 − z1)/2π)² · p/C, rounded up to the next even number of pitches because a full link spans two. The centres for that link count come back from C = (p/8)(2L − z1 − z2 + √((2L − z1 − z2)² − 8((z2 − z1)/π)²)). This is geometry only: it does not rate the chain for power, so check the manufacturer's power, wear and lubrication data before building or loading the drive. Everything is computed in this browser: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
