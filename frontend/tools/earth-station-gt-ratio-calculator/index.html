Where the noise temperature comes from

The feed is a lossy two-port with loss ratio α = 10L/10. It attenuates the antenna noise to Ta/α and adds (1 − 1/α)Tphys of its own, so the system noise temperature at the LNA input is Tsys = Ta/α + (1 − 1/α)Tphys + TR, with TR = 290 (10NF/10 − 1). G/T is then the receive gain minus 10 log10(Tsys), and the figure is the same whether it is referenced to the antenna port or to the receiver input. Only the clear-sky receive chain is modelled: atmospheric and precipitation noise, antenna spillover and any availability margin are not included. Everything is calculated in this page; nothing is sent or stored.
