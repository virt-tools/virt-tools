Round Robin Scheduler

Build a fixture list where every team plays every other team, once or home and away. Byes are handled and home advantage stays balanced, so no team gets a lopsided run. Runs locally in your browser.

Enter the number of teams to build a schedule.

Fixtures come from the circle method, so every pair meets exactly once per leg and an odd team count gives each team one bye. The first-named team is at home; home games are spread as evenly as the round structure allows, and a double round robin reverses them so every team finishes with the same number of home games. Teams are unseeded — no strength balancing, travel, venue or date assignment.
