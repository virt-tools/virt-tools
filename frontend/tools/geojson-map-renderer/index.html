GeoJSON Map Renderer

Paste a GeoJSON document and draw it as a self-contained SVG map: automatic bounds, Web Mercator or equirectangular projection, an optional graticule, and markup you can copy or download.

FeatureCollection, Feature, geometry or GeometryCollection, with WGS84 longitudes and latitudes
EPSG:3857, or plate carrée
240 to 1600 px, height 75%
degree lines and edge labels
Paste GeoJSON and render it to see the map.

Coordinates are read as WGS84 longitude and latitude in degrees, the order RFC 7946 specifies. Geometry is drawn as SVG paths and points as circles: no map tiles, basemaps or place labels are fetched, because the page makes no network requests at all. Features that cross the antimeridian are unwrapped so the drawing stays in one piece rather than being cut at ±180°, and latitudes beyond ±85.05° are clamped for Web Mercator, which cannot show the poles. Properties, styling and elevation are ignored; this is a quick drawing of a file, not a cartographic or survey product. The markup you copy is a standalone SVG with its own background, so it opens without this site. Nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.
