Working time is counted minute by minute inside a repeating daily window: a day contributes only the part of that window that falls between the start and the end, and only if it is one of the chosen weekdays and not listed as a holiday. Weekends, nights, lunch breaks and holidays are simply outside the window, so nothing is subtracted twice. Times are wall-clock times on your own device, so a daylight-saving day still counts as its scheduled length rather than 23 or 25 hours, and the window must end after it starts, so an overnight shift needs two ranges. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
