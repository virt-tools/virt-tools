Data Transfer Rate Converter

Convert a transfer rate between bit and byte units per second, with decimal SI prefixes and binary IEC prefixes side by side.

A transfer rate is a quantity per second, so a byte unit is simply eight bits: 100 Mbit/s and 12.5 MB/s are the same rate. Decimal prefixes (k, M, G, T) step by 1,000 and are the normal choice for network rates; binary prefixes (Ki, Mi, Gi, Ti) step by 1,024 and describe byte rates measured by an operating system. These are nominal unit conversions: protocol overhead, framing, forward error correction and retransmissions all reduce the payload that actually moves, so a link does not carry a file at its full line rate. Values are rounded to six significant digits and very small or very large ones are shown in exponent form. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
