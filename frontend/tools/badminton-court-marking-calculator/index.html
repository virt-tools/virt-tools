Court and paint

BWF Law 1.1 fixes every court line at 40 mm, so 4 cm is the compliant width. The paint figures use the width you enter, one width for every line.

Take the spreading rate from the paint tin, usually 6 to 12 m² per litre for a court line paint. The result is one coat.

The singles side lines sit 0.46 m inside the doubles side lines and do not shorten the 13.40 m court.
