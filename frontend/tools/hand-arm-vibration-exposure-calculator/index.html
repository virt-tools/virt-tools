Hand-Arm Vibration Exposure Calculator

Add up the hand-arm vibration from the powered tools you use in a day and see the daily exposure A(8) and the HSE exposure points total. The result is compared with the 100-point exposure action value (2.5 m/s² A(8)) and the 400-point exposure limit value (5 m/s² A(8)) used in the Control of Vibration at Work Regulations 2005. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    What this does not do

    • It uses the vibration magnitude you enter, normally the value the manufacturer declares for the tool. Real exposure depends on the tool's condition, the accessory fitted, the material worked, grip and technique.
    • It covers hand-arm vibration from hand-held and hand-guided tools only. Whole-body vibration from driving or standing on a vibrating platform is a separate exposure with different values.
    • It is arithmetic on your own entries, not a risk assessment. Under the Control of Vibration at Work Regulations 2005 an employer must assess the risk, not just the number, and the daily exposure must be kept below the limit value.
    • Nothing is saved, no account is needed and no device sensor is read: reloading the page clears the form.
