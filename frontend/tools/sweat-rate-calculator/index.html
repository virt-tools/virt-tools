The weigh-in method compares body mass before and after the session. Mass lost is treated as fluid lost at about 1 kg per litre, and the drink taken on is added back while any urine passed is taken off, so sweat loss = weight lost + fluid drunk − urine passed. That volume divided by the session length gives the hourly rate. Sweat rates vary widely between people and with heat, clothing and effort — typical values often quoted are roughly 0.5 to 2 litres per hour, and a single session is only one sample. This is arithmetic on your own measurements, not medical advice or a drinking prescription; follow event or clinician guidance for anything beyond planning. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
