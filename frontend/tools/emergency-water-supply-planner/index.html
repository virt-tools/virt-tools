Emergency Water Supply Planner

Enter how many people you are planning for, how many days the supply must cover and how much water you already store. You get the volume you need in gallons and liters, the split between drinking and other uses, how many days your stored water lasts, and the number of jugs, bottles or cases that close the gap. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

Household and supply

Count everyone who will drink and wash from this supply, from 1 to 12.

    What this does not do

    • The one gallon per person per day figure is a household planning baseline for drinking and sanitation. Hot weather, hard physical work, pregnancy, illness, nursing and pets all raise the real need, and nothing here adjusts for them.
    • Container counts round up to whole containers, so the capacity you buy is usually slightly more than the requirement. Shapes and stacking are not considered.
    • Water you fill yourself should be replaced every six months; commercially bottled water carries its own date. This page shows the six-month date but does not track, remind or store anything.
    • It plans quantities only. It gives no treatment, storage or medical advice, and reloading the page clears your entries.

    Basic kit checklist

    From the Ready.gov Build A Kit list, for reference alongside the water plan.

    • Water, one gallon per person per day for several days
    • Food, at least a several-day supply of non-perishable items
    • Battery-powered or hand-crank radio and a NOAA Weather Radio
    • Flashlight and extra batteries
    • First aid kit
    • Whistle to signal for help
    • Dust mask
    • Plastic sheeting, scissors and duct tape
    • Moist towelettes, garbage bags and plastic ties
    • Wrench or pliers to turn off utilities
    • Manual can opener
    • Local maps
    • Cell phone with chargers and a backup battery
