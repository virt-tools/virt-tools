The conversion scales pace by the relative energy cost of running on a slope. Cost per kilogram per metre is taken from Minetti's published running curve, 155.4i⁵ − 30.4i⁴ − 43.3i³ + 46.3i² + 19.5i + 3.6, where i is the gradient as a fraction; flat running costs 3.6. A pace on a grade is multiplied by flat cost ÷ grade cost, and the reverse direction divides instead, so both directions undo each other exactly. Grades are limited to ±25% and paces to 2:00–30:00 per km or 3:15–48:00 per mile, outside which the curve is an extrapolation. The model ignores wind, surface, altitude, heat, technique and fatigue, so treat the result as an adjusted training reference rather than a prediction or a race plan. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
