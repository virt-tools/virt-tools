    Each step around the Camelot wheel is a perfect fifth, so the number next to a key is a fourth or fifth away and the other letter in the same slice is its relative major or minor. Three moves stay in key: the same code, one number up or down, or the same number with the other letter. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
