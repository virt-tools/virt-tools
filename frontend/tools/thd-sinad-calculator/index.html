THD & SINAD Calculator

Turn a fundamental level and the levels of its harmonics and noise floor into total harmonic distortion, THD+N, SINAD and effective bits. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Signal levels

The tone you are measuring, as an RMS level against full scale.

The RMS noise with the tone muted. Leave blank to measure distortion only.

Harmonic levels

Each harmonic of the fundamental, as an RMS level in dBFS. Leave a harmonic blank when it is not present or below the analyser's floor.
