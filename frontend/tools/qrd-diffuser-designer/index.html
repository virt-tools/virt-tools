QRD Diffuser Designer

Size a one-dimensional quadratic-residue diffuser from its design frequency, upper frequency and prime well count: every well depth, the clear well width, the panel width and the deepest well.

The design frequency sets the well depths. The upper frequency sets the clear well width: a well wider than half a wavelength there reflects like a flat panel. Dimensions are millimetres, with the speed of sound fixed at 343 m/s (dry air at 20 °C).

Residues (n² mod N)

The residue sequence is sn = n² mod N for n = 0 to N − 1, and each well is sn × λ0/(2N) deep, where λ0 is the wavelength at the design frequency, so the deepest well follows the largest residue rather than a fixed fraction of the sequence. The clear well width is half a wavelength at the upper frequency, the widest well that still scatters instead of reflecting specularly. Widths count the clear openings only: the dividers between wells and the frame add material, and the depths sit behind the front face. This page returns a geometry, not a scattering prediction — mounting, surface material and edge treatment change the result, and a diffuser complements absorption rather than replacing it.
