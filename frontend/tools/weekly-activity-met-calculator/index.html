Weekly Activity MET Calculator

Add up a typical week the way the World Health Organization's Global Physical Activity Questionnaire does: work, travel and leisure, each counted in MET-minutes. The result shows how much of your volume comes from each domain and whether the total reaches the 600 MET-minute recommendation. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    What this does not do

    • It is arithmetic on the ten numbers you enter, not a measurement. Recall of a typical week is approximate, and the answer is only as good as those entries.
    • GPAQ fixes the intensity values at 8 METs for vigorous and 4 METs for moderate activity rather than looking up each activity in a compendium, so this is a surveillance-style volume, not a calorie count.
    • Sitting time, strength training, steps and activities shorter than 10 minutes do not enter the total. Nothing is saved: reloading the page clears the form, and weight, heart rate and device data are not used.
    • It is a planning and tracking aid, not medical advice. If you have a condition that limits activity, or you are starting after a long break, get qualified advice about what is safe for you.
