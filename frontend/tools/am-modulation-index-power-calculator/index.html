Enter the carrier power and depth to see the modulated power.

The carrier never changes with depth, so only the sidebands add power: a 100 W carrier at 50 % modulation becomes 112.5 W total, with 12.5 W in the two sidebands and 11.1 % of the total doing useful work. Peak envelope power is the crest of the envelope, four times the carrier at 100 %, which is the figure a transmitter is normally rated by. Depths above 100 % are computed and flagged rather than refused, because that is what an overdriven modulator produces, but a standard envelope detector will clip them. The inverse row uses a measured total power to back out the depth it implies. A single steady tone and a linear modulator are assumed; speech crest factor, ALC, suppressed carrier and SSB are not modelled.
