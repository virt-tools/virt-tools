    What this does not do

    • Every element is ideal and lossless. Real inductors have winding resistance, a self-resonant frequency and a Q that falls with frequency; at UHF and above those set the response long before the ideal curve here does.
    • The ladder is doubly terminated in the impedance you enter, so reactance is absorbed at both ends. A different load moves the ripple, and component tolerances of a few percent are enough to raise a Chebyshev ripple and shift the cutoff.
    • It designs the standard all-pole Butterworth and Chebyshev low-pass prototypes only. Elliptic, inverse-Chebyshev and Bessel responses, band-pass and high-pass transforms, and impedance-transforming ends are not modelled.
    • Nothing is saved and nothing is uploaded: reloading the page clears the fields.
