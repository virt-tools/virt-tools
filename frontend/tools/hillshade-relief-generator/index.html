Hillshade Relief Generator

Paste an elevation grid and light it from a sun of your choosing: the page computes slope and aspect over a 3 by 3 window and draws a shaded-relief image, with the slope statistics and the ground area the grid covers.

Elevation grid

One row per line, one elevation per column, in metres, separated by spaces, commas or semicolons. At least 3 rows and 3 columns, at most 64 of each. The outer rows and columns have no full 3 by 3 window, so they stay blank.

Your browser will not draw the relief image, but the figures below are still computed.

Dark is away from the sun, light faces it: 0 is fully shaded and 255 is fully lit. The hatched margin is the edge of the grid, where a full 3 by 3 window does not fit.

Figures

A hillshade is an illumination model, not a terrain survey: it shows how the surface would be lit from a chosen sun position, and it inherits every error in the elevations you paste. Azimuth is measured clockwise from north, altitude from the horizon, and the vertical exaggeration multiplies each gradient before the slope is taken. Everything is computed in the browser: no storage, uploads or network access.
