Bike Trail & Fork Geometry Calculator

Enter a bicycle's wheel size, head angle and fork offset to get its steering trail — the distance the front tire's contact patch sits behind the steering axis — together with normal trail and wheel flop. It also works backwards: the fork offset that would give a trail you name. Pure geometry from the wheel and fork; everything runs in your browser and nothing is stored.

    What this does not do

    • Trail, normal trail and wheel flop come from static geometry on a rigid fork. Suspension travel, tire compression, headset angle changes and load all move the real numbers, so treat the result as the design value, not a measurement.
    • It describes one steering dimension only. Wheelbase, mass distribution, tire profile, frame stiffness and steering damping also shape how a bike handles, so the numbers here are no verdict on whether a bike is right for you or safe to ride.
    • Motorcycle rake is measured from the vertical, which is the complement of the bicycle head angle used here; enter the head angle or the result will be wrong.
    • Nothing is saved and nothing is uploaded: reloading the page clears the fields.
