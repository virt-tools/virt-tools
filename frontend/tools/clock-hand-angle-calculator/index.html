The hour hand turns 0.5° a minute and the minute hand 6° a minute, so the gap between them grows by 5.5° a minute; seconds add 1/120° and 0.1°. Angles are measured clockwise from 12, and the angle between the hands is the shorter of the two arcs, so it never exceeds 180°. The hands line up 11 times in 12 hours and point opposite ways another 11 times; for any other angle there are 22 moments in the 12-hour cycle. Times are shown on the face and repeat every 12 hours. This models an ideal face, not a real movement's tick or lost seconds. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
