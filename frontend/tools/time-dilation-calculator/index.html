Time Dilation Calculator

Work out the Lorentz factor and how much time passes for the traveller on a trip at a fraction of the speed of light.

At a constant speed v the clock on board a ship runs slower than one left behind by the Lorentz factor γ = 1 / √(1 − v²/c²). The trip takes distance / v as measured on Earth and that many years divided by γ as measured on board, while the crew also measures the distance as shortened by the same factor. Distances are given in light-years, so a trip of 4.24 light-years at 90% of c lasts 4.711 years on Earth and 2.053 years for the traveller. The calculation assumes constant speed and ignores the acceleration at each end, so it is the textbook special-relativity result, not a flight plan. A speed of 100% of c or more is refused because nothing with mass reaches it. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
