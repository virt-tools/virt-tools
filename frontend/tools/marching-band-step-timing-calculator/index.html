Marching Band Step & Timing Calculator

An 8-to-5 step is 22.5 inches, so eight counts carry a marcher 5 yards. Pick the step plan, the tempo and the counts to get the distance, the time on the field and the ground speed, then the counts each field span needs.

A plan is the number of steps a marcher takes to cover 5 yards, so the step is 180 inches divided by that number: 8-to-5 is 22.5 in, 6-to-5 is 30 in, 12-to-5 is 15 in and 16-to-5 is 11.25 in. Counts are steps, one step per count. Field spans use the 100 yard by 53⅓ yard playing field.

Step sizes are measured heel to heel. The plans are definitions, not estimates: every one is a fixed number of equal steps to 5 yards, which is why 8-to-5 is exactly 22.5 inches and 16-to-5 exactly half of it. A count is one step when the band steps on every count, as drill is normally written; a move held in half notes takes two counts per step, so enter the steps, not the note values. The field spans use the playing field between the goal lines, 100 yards long and 53⅓ yards wide; the end zones and the space outside the sidelines are not included. Whole counts round the span up, because a marcher cannot take part of a step. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
