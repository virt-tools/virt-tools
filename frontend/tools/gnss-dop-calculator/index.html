GNSS Dilution of Precision Calculator

Compute PDOP, HDOP, VDOP, GDOP and TDOP from the directions of the satellites your receiver can see, apply an elevation mask, and see how much the sky geometry amplifies range error. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.

Visible satellites

One satellite per line: azimuth in degrees clockwise from true north, then elevation in degrees above the horizon. Separators may be commas or spaces; up to 64 satellites.

Satellites below the mask are excluded, as a real receiver would exclude them.

A planning assumption for the error on each satellite range; 5 m is typical of a consumer receiver.
