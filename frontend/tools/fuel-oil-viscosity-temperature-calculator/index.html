Enter two viscosity-temperature points to see the fit.

The fit is the ASTM D341 Walther form: log10(log10(ν + 0.7)) = A − B log10(T), with the kinematic viscosity ν in cSt and the temperature T in kelvin. Two measured points fix A and B, so the line passes through both readings; everything else is read off that line. The form is used above 2 cSt, where the standard adds no low-viscosity correction, so this page refuses values below 2 cSt. It describes a fuel that has been tested at two temperatures — an extrapolation far beyond those points, or a single laboratory result interpreted as a certificate, is not a substitute for a measured viscosity at the working temperature. Everything is calculated in this page: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
