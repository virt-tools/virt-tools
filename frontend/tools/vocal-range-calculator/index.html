Standard voice ranges

    Notes are read as scientific pitch, where middle C is C4 and A4 is the 440 Hz reference. Frequencies follow 12-tone equal temperament, so each semitone multiplies the pitch by 21/12 and one octave doubles it. The voice types are the common classical and choral reference ranges, and they are approximate: classifications differ between traditions, choral sections and Fach systems, and a range on one day is not a diagnosis. Head voice, falsetto, passaggio and comfortable tessitura are not modelled, so treat a match as a comparison rather than a verdict. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
