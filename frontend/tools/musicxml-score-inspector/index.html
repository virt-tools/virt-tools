MusicXML Score Inspector

Structural inspection only—not score rendering, playback, notation interpretation, performance guidance, or correctness certification. Concert pitch is not derived: pitch ranges are written pitch; transposition elements are reported as metadata.

Policy: uncompressed MusicXML score-partwise only. score-timewise and compressed .mxl are rejected. DOCTYPE/entity declarations are rejected before DOMParser; no external content is requested.
