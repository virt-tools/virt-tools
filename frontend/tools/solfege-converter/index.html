Ascending from the tonic. Descending, the chromatic steps read ti–te–la–le–sol–se–fa–mi–me–re–ra–do.

Solfège names scale degrees with syllables. Fixed do ties each syllable to a pitch, so do is C everywhere; movable do ties syllables to the key, so do is the tonic of a major key; la-based minor starts on la and keeps do on the relative major's tonic. Notes outside the key take the chromatic syllables di, ri, fi, si and li when rising, and ra, me, se, le and te when falling. This page uses the English syllables, with ti for the leading tone and si for the raised fifth — the continental use of si for B is not followed here. Input is a space- or comma-separated sequence of up to 32 items; octave numbers on notes are ignored, so the result is a pitch-class name rather than a register. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
