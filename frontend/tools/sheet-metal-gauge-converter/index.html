Sheet Metal Gauge Converter

16 gauge is 1.519 mm in carbon steel, 1.613 mm galvanized, 1.587 mm stainless and 1.290 mm in aluminum. Convert a gauge to the thickness of the metal you are buying, or enter a thickness and read the nearest gauge in each of the four tables.

A whole number is read as a gauge (16, 16 ga or gauge 16); anything with a unit or a decimal point is read as a thickness (1.52 mm, 0.0598 in).

A gauge number is not a thickness: it comes from a weight per square foot in each metal’s own gauge system, so the same number is a different sheet in every column. Values here are nominal decimal inches converted at exactly 25.4 mm per inch. Mills roll within a tolerance, coated and polished sheet varies, and some suppliers quote gauge from their own table, so check the mill certificate or a micrometer before cutting a critical part.
