Warm-Up Set Planner

Turn a working weight into an empty-bar, 40, 60, 80 and 90 percent warm-up ladder with reps, rounded to weights your smallest plate pair can actually build.

The ladder is the common strength-training ramp of an empty bar for five, then five reps at 40%, three at 60%, two at 80% and one at 90% of the working weight. Every rung is rounded to the nearest weight the bar can actually reach, which is the bar plus a multiple of twice the smallest plate pair; a rung that rounds onto the load below it is dropped, and the count of dropped rungs is reported. Because the smallest pair limits what can be loaded, the working weight itself is rounded the same way and any difference is shown. Percentages are a coaching convention, not a prescription: adjust reps and rungs to the lifter, the exercise and how the bar feels. This is a planning aid for healthy lifters, not medical or rehabilitation advice, and everything runs locally in your browser with no storage or network access.
