Spice Blend Formulator

Salt and sugar contributions are arithmetic estimates based only on percentages you enter. They are not nutrition, health, dietary, or medical advice. Verify ingredient specifications and suitability independently.

Parts require “parts”; percentages require “%”. For mass basis, g/kg/oz/lb convert directly; ml/L/tsp/tbsp/cup require a positive user-entered density in g/ml. Cost is entered per gram; cost, salt, and sugar estimates use exact pre-rounding masses. No volume-to-mass conversion is inferred.
