Audio Phase Alignment Explorer

Diagnostic comparison only—not mastering, loudspeaker alignment, acoustic measurement, or certification. Results depend on range, signal energy, window, noise, periodicity, and the stated delay convention.

WAV policy: RIFF/WAVE PCM 8/16/24/32-bit or IEEE float 32-bit only, ≤20 MiB and ≤1,000,000 decoded samples per source. Sample rates must match; no resampling is performed. Positive lag means B occurs later: correlation pairs A[i] with B[i+lag], so preview alignment advances B by that integer lag.
