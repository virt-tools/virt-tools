The model counts 8 preamble/SFD bytes, the 14-byte header, any 802.1Q tags, the 4-byte FCS, 12 inter-frame-gap bytes and padding to the 64-byte minimum, then divides the line rate by the resulting wire size. It assumes a saturated link with no collisions, pause frames, frame preemption, FEC or 64b/66b line coding — 10GBASE-R, for example, runs at 10.3125 Gbaud — and no protocol, congestion or latency behaviour. Figures are theoretical maxima, not measured throughput. Nothing leaves your browser.
