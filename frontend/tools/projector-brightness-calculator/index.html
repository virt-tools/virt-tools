Projector Brightness Calculator

Turn projector lumens into screen luminance for your screen size and gain, or work back from a brightness target to the lumens you need.

Screen luminance is the light the screen reflects back to the audience. The calculator splits the diagonal into width and height for the chosen aspect ratio, takes the screen area, spreads the projector's lumens over it, and multiplies by the screen gain, where gain 1.0 is a plain matte white surface that reflects light evenly in every direction. One foot-lambert is 1/π candela per square foot, so 14 fL is 48 cd/m²; the three targets are the DCI cinema value of 14 fL, the 16 fL that 35 mm film prints were graded for, and the 7 fL of legacy lamp-based 3D. The result is the centre-of-screen figure: screens lose brightness towards the corners, and lens, port glass and lamp ageing all reduce it, so treat it as a best case. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
