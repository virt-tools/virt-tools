Wi-Fi Access Point Capacity Planner

Size a floor's Wi-Fi from its area, peak client count and the throughput each client needs.

peak clients: laptops, phones and IoT

Enter the floor and client figures.

The planner takes the larger of two counts. For capacity, the aggregate demand is the device count times the target throughput per device, and it is divided by the usable throughput of one AP after removing the headroom, then rounded up. For coverage, the floor area is divided by the coverage area of one AP and rounded up. Headroom is held back as spare airtime, so a plan sized by capacity runs at 100% minus the headroom when every client asks for its full target. The figures are a first-pass budget: real cell size depends on walls, ceilings, antenna patterns and a site survey, and usable throughput depends on the band, channel width, spatial streams, client mix and interference, so measure before committing to an installation. Everything is calculated in the browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
