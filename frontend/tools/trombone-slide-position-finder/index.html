Scientific pitch notation: a letter, an optional sharp or flat, and the octave, for example F4, Bb3 or C#4. Middle C is C4. A trombone part is written at sounding pitch.

Reference chart: partials 1 to 8 in every position

Partial 1 is the pedal tone, which is hard to produce and rarely used. Cells marked * are more than 20 cents from the equal-tempered note: the seventh partial is about 31 cents flat, so it is played with a shortened slide. On a narrow screen the table scrolls sideways.

Each position out adds one semitone, so a note can sit in more than one. Positions are not identical on every instrument and the charts disagree slightly on the shortened alternates: tune the slide by ear. The page covers standard positions only; it does not model a bass trombone's second valve or a trigger tuned down to E. Everything is computed in the browser: no storage, uploads or network access.
