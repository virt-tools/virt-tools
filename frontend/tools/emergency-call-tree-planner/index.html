Emergency Call Tree Planner

Turn a list of names into a telephone tree for emergency notifications: who calls whom, how many calls it takes, how deep the tree is and how long the last person waits. The first name is the coordinator who starts the chain. Everything is calculated in your browser; no phone numbers are collected, nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored.

Your list

One person per line, at most 60. The first line is the coordinator; the rest are handed out level by level, so the tree stays balanced.

    What this does not do

    • It plans the calling order only. No calls are made, no phone numbers are used and no message is sent.
    • Timing assumes every call in a tier starts together and reaches the person after the entered call length. One unanswered call delays that whole branch, so a real plan needs a fallback for each caller.
    • People without a phone, or who also have to call the emergency services, need a separate arrangement; the tree does not model them.
    • Nothing is saved and no account is needed: reloading the page clears the box.
