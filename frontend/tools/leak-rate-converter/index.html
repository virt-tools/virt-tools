Leak Rate Converter

Convert a gas leak rate — a pressure-times-volume throughput, pV/t — between the SI unit and the vacuum, instrument and US units that leak specifications still use.

A leak rate is a gas throughput, pV/t: a leak of 1 mbar·L/s raises the pressure of a one-litre volume by 1 mbar every second. Every factor follows from a definition — 1 atm = 101 325 Pa, 1 L = 0.001 m³, 1 Torr = 101 325/760 Pa — so none of them comes from a measurement. NIST's gas-flow table assumes an ideal gas at 101 325 Pa and 0 °C for the units written with “atm”, and some instruments quote sccm at 25 °C instead; the pV-per-time conversion here is the same either way, but turning it into a mass flow needs that reference state.
