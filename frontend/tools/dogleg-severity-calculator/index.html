Dogleg Severity Calculator

Two survey stations in, the dogleg angle and severity out: minimum curvature gives the turn between the stations, the degrees per 30 m and per 100 ft, the ratio factor, and the north, east and TVD increments.

Both measured depths use this unit. Inclination is measured from vertical and azimuth clockwise from north, both in degrees. The severity is always reported in the two standard reference lengths.

The minimum curvature method treats the wellbore between two stations as a circular arc. With the two inclinations I and azimuths A in radians, the dogleg angle is β = 2 arcsin √(sin²((I2−I1)/2) + sin I1 sin I2 sin²((A2−A1)/2)); the ratio factor is (2/β) tan(β/2), which tends to 1 as β tends to zero; the increments are ΔTVD = (ΔMD/2)(cos I1 + cos I2) RF, ΔN = (ΔMD/2)(sin I1 cos A1 + sin I2 cos A2) RF and ΔE = (ΔMD/2)(sin I1 sin A1 + sin I2 sin A2) RF; and dogleg severity = β × 30 / ΔMD in metres, or β × 100 / ΔMD in feet, with β in degrees. The severity is an average over the interval: it depends on how far apart the stations are, and it says nothing about where inside the interval the turn happened. Only two stations are used, so a multi-station survey is reduced one interval at a time, and nothing here checks the survey quality, the well plan or the drill string's limits. Everything is computed in this browser: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
