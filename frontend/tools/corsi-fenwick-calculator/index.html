How the shot attempts add up
CountForAgainstDiff

Corsi counts every shot attempt at even strength: shots on goal, missed shots and blocked attempts, added separately for and against. Corsi For % is CF divided by CF plus CA, so 50 % is an even share. Fenwick drops the blocked attempts and keeps shots on goal plus misses. Shooting percentage is goals divided by shots on goal, save percentage is saves divided by shots on goal against, and PDO is the two added together — 100 is break-even, and a single game’s PDO is mostly noise. Every figure is descriptive: Corsi approximates possession without weighing shot quality or location, and small samples swing widely. Nothing is sent or stored.
