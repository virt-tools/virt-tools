Enter a finished runtime and either the footage shot or a target ratio.

Shooting ratio is recorded footage divided by finished runtime, so 10:1 means ten hours shot for every finished hour. It counts material recorded, not camera cards or takes: unused footage is what was shot beyond the length of the cut, and the usable share is the runtime divided by the footage. Actual ratios depend on the production — unscripted and documentary work usually runs higher than tightly scripted scenes — so treat any target as a planning figure. Durations are whole seconds; the result is rounded to the nearest second. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
