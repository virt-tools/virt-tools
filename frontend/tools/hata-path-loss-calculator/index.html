Okumura-Hata Path Loss Calculator

The Okumura-Hata model is the workhorse for VHF and UHF land-mobile planning: give it a frequency, a base and a mobile antenna height and a distance, and it returns the median path loss. The COST-231 form extends the same equations to 2 GHz. This page shows the loss, how each term built it, and how far it sits above free space.

Link parameters

COST-231 adds the 3 dB metropolitan term; below 1500 MHz use Okumura-Hata.

COST-231 has no open/rural form, and treats a suburb as C_m = 0 dB.

Antenna heights are metres above local ground, distance is kilometres along the path, and the result is median loss — not a guarantee for one particular street.

    What the equations do

    1. Okumura-Hata. Median urban loss is 69.55 + 26.16 log₁₀ f − 13.82 log₁₀ h_b − a(h_m) + (44.9 − 6.55 log₁₀ h_b) log₁₀ d with f in MHz, h_b and h_m in metres and d in kilometres.
    2. The mobile correction a(h_m). A medium–small city uses (1.1 log₁₀ f − 0.7) h_m − (1.56 log₁₀ f − 0.8). A large city swaps in 8.29 (log₁₀ 1.54 h_m)² − 1.1 at 200 MHz and below, or 3.2 (log₁₀ 11.75 h_m)² − 4.97 at 400 MHz and above.
    3. Suburban takes the Okumura-Hata medium-city loss less 2 (log₁₀ f/28)² + 5.4 dB; open / rural takes it less 4.78 (log₁₀ f)² − 18.33 log₁₀ f + 40.94 dB.
    4. COST-231 Hata keeps the shape but replaces the constant with 46.3 and the frequency term with 33.9 log₁₀ f, then adds C_m = 3 dB for a dense urban centre and 0 dB for a medium city or suburb.
    5. Free space is 32.45 + 20 log₁₀ f + 20 log₁₀ d dB, the same units; the difference is what terrain, buildings and the low mobile antenna cost you.

    Validity. Okumura-Hata is fitted for 150–1500 MHz, base heights of 30–200 m, mobile heights of 1–10 m and distances of 1–20 km; COST-231 for 1500–2000 MHz over the same geometry. The page refuses inputs outside those ranges instead of extrapolating. Measured loss scatters by several dB around these medians, and neither model knows about terrain profiles, foliage, building penetration, antenna patterns, feeder loss or the difference between a rooftop site and a mast below the skyline. For microcells, indoor paths, street canyons and links shorter than a kilometre, use a model built for them.
