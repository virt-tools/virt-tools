Measure the rings across their outside edge, and the height straight down between the rings. A drum shade ignores the top diameter.

The cover is one trapezoid per panel: its bottom edge is the bottom ring's circumference, its top edge the top ring's, and its height is the slant height — the straight height for a drum, or the square root of the height squared plus half the difference in diameters squared for an empire shade. Edge allowances are added top and bottom and seam allowances at every side. Because fabric comes in fixed widths, the page splits the shade into the smallest number of equal panels that fit across the usable width, puts a seam between each pair and works out how many lengths of fabric that takes. Lining needs the same yardage as the cover. A pattern repeat, nap or directional print, fabric shrinkage and the stretch of a bias-cut panel are not modelled, so buy a little extra when the print has to be matched at the seams.
