Heat Flux Density Converter

Convert a heat flux density — the power crossing one square metre of surface — between the SI units and the Btu and calorie units that older handbooks and US building data still use.

One Btu/(h·ft²) is 3.154591 W/m² and one cal/(s·cm²) is 41,840 W/m². The Btu is the International Table Btu of 1055.05585262 J, the calorie the thermochemical calorie of 4.184 J, and the foot and inch the international foot of 0.3048 m and inch of 0.0254 m, so every factor here follows from a definition rather than a measurement. Heat flux density is a rate per area, not a total: multiply by the area for a heat flow rate in watts. Everything runs in your browser.
