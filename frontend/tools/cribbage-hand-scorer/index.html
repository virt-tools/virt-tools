Cribbage Hand Scorer

Count a four-card cribbage hand with its starter card, point by point. Runs locally in your browser.

A four-card flush scores nothing in the crib; only five cards in one suit count.

Pick four cards and the starter.

How this adds up

  • Scoring combinations appear here.

Fifteens score 2 points for every distinct combination totalling 15; aces count 1 and face cards 10. Each pair scores 2. Only the longest run counts, once per distinct ordering, so 4-4-5-6 with a 7 cut is a double run of four (10 for the runs plus the pair). A flush is 4 in hand, 5 with the starter, and in the crib only 5. His nobs is 1 for a jack in hand matching the starter suit. His heels (2 for a cut jack) and pegging are not included.
