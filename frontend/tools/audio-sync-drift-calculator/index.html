Drift grows linearly: a 1,000 ppm mismatch is 0.1% of the running time, so a 20-minute take finishes 1.2 seconds apart. Whether sound ends up early or late depends on which clock runs fast, so the correction row gives both directions. Re-sync spacing is the allowed drift divided by the mismatch and the frame rate.
