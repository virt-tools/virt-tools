Enter the rug size to see knots, strand length and skeins.

Knots follow the canvas: the width and length are rounded to the nearest whole holes, so a 24 in side on 3.3 mesh is 79 holes. Each knot is one strand folded through a hole, so the cut length is twice the pile height plus 0.5 in to wrap the canvas thread — 2.5 in strands for a 1 in pile. Results are a buying estimate, not a pattern: knot count assumes every hole is filled, waste is the trimming allowance, and yarn thickness, dye lot and your own tension are not modelled. Check the yardage on the skein label before ordering. Nothing is saved or sent anywhere.
