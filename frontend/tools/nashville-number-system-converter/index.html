Diatonic numbers

NumberChordTriad

Numbers are the degrees of the major scale of the key, so 1 is the tonic chord: in C, 1 4 5 means C F G. Roots outside the key take a flat or sharp in front of the number, so B♭ in C is b7 and F♯ is b5 (write #4 if you prefer the sharp spelling). Chord qualities are copied through as written — Am7 in C becomes 6m7 — and a slash bass note is numbered too, so C/E is 1/3. For a minor-key song, choose its relative major: Am–F–C–G in C reads 6m 4 1 5. Six-nine chords are written 6add9 because a slash always means a bass note.
