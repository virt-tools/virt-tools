    What this does not do

    • It counts no container, file-system or clip overhead and no proxy files, so real recording time is a few percent shorter than the rounded figure here. Leave a margin on the last card.
    • The bitrate is taken as one constant rate for the whole shoot. Variable-bitrate codecs drift either way, and audio is assumed to be inside the bitrate you enter.
    • Offload time uses the reader's rated read speed. Readers and the drive behind them rarely sustain it, and extra copies add write time on top of the single read pass.
    • It plans media only. It reads no card, checks no camera's approved media list and keeps no record: reloading the page clears your entries.
