Model length = real length ÷ N, so a 12 m object at 1:72 is 167 mm long. Areas scale by N² and volumes and masses by N³: the same 1:72 model has 1/5,184 of the surface and 1/373,248 of the volume of the real object. The scale is only as good as the two lengths you give it, and a kit labelled 1:72 is often a little over- or undersized.
