Enter the waist and hole layout to see the belt plan.

Belt size is measured from the fold at the buckle to the centre hole, so the centre hole sits your waist plus the fit allowance from the fold. Holes step outwards from that centre hole at the spacing you enter, and the tip runs on past the last hole by the tip allowance, so the finished belt is the centre hole plus half the hole span plus the tip. The strap blank adds the fold-back that wraps round the buckle bar. If you own a belt that fits, measure it from the fold to the hole you use and enter that as the waist with a zero allowance. This is a cutting plan for leather straps, not a fitting guarantee: mark the holes and punch them on scrap first. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
