Specific Energy Converter

Convert energy per mass - the energy stored in a kilogram of fuel, food or battery - and see every supported unit at once.

Add a mass to turn the specific energy into a total energy in MJ and kWh.

Specific energy is energy divided by mass, so its SI unit is the joule per kilogram (J/kg); energy density, in J/m3, is a different quantity that also needs the density. One kilowatt hour is 3.6 megajoules, so 1 kWh/kg is 3.6 MJ/kg, and one megajoule per kilogram is one gigajoule per tonne. The calorie here is the thermochemical calorie of 4.184 J, the one used for food energy, and the BTU is the International Table BTU of 1055.05585262 J with the avoirdupois pound of 0.45359237 kg, so 1 Btu/lb is exactly 2326 J/kg. These are unit conversions only: a fuel's lower and higher heating values differ, a battery's usable energy is below its cell-level value, and no combustion, efficiency or density model is applied. Values are rounded to six significant digits, and very small or very large ones are shown in exponent form. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
