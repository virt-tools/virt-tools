RIAA Equalization Calculator

Read the RIAA disc equalization curve at any frequency: the playback boost or cut in decibels relative to 1 kHz, the same figure reversed for the record (cut) side, the amplitude ratio, and the gain a magnetic cartridge needs to reach a line level. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded or sent anywhere.
