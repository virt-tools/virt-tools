One nit is one candela per square metre. The foot-lambert is one candela per square foot divided by π, and one candela per square foot is 10.7639104 cd/m², so 1 fL is about 3.4262591 cd/m². The lambert is one candela per square centimetre divided by π and the apostilb is one candela per square metre divided by π, which makes 1 L about 3183.099 cd/m² and 1 asb about 0.3183099 cd/m². Luminance is light leaving or reflecting off a surface in a given direction; illuminance, in lux, is light arriving at it, and the two are related by reflectance. Display "nits" are peak figures measured over a small patch at full white, so a panel rated 1000 nits usually shows far less across a full-screen image. The table values are rounded orders of magnitude, not specifications, and nothing here establishes a legibility or safety rating. Results are rounded to six significant digits.
