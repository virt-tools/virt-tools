A scan format fixes four numbers: active samples per line, active lines, samples per total line including blanking, and total lines per frame. The line rate is the pixel clock divided by the samples per total line, and the frame rate is that line rate divided by the total lines, so 1080p at 25 Hz has 2,640 samples in a 35.556 µs line and 1,125 lines in a 40 ms frame. Interlaced formats carry two fields per frame at twice the frame rate. Fractional rates such as 59.94 are the 60 Hz timing divided by 1.001. Values are nominal: sync widths, tolerances and ancillary data are not modelled. Everything is calculated in this page and nothing is uploaded or stored.
