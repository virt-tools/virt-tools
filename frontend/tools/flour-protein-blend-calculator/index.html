Blending is a straight mass balance: the protein in the two parts adds up, and dividing it by the final weight gives the blend's percentage. Nothing creates protein, so the target has to sit between the two flours — use a stronger flour or vital wheat gluten above them, or a weaker flour or starch below. Bag percentages are rounded by the miller and vary by brand and crop, so treat the answer as a starting point and adjust the dough by feel; vital wheat gluten is typically about 75% protein but check the label. Weights are in grams, rounded to 0.1 g, which can move the last decimal of the blend percentage. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
