Badminton Scorekeeper

Tap the side that won each rally. Keep the score, serving side and game results through a best-of-three match.

Game 1 · Side A serves from the right service court.

Completed games

Every rally scores a point. Games are to 21 with a two-point lead from 20-all, capped at 30; the match is best of three. The rally winner serves next, and the game winner serves first in the next game. Change ends after games one and two, and at 11 points in game three. Side-level doubles scoring is supported, but individual doubles server rotation is not tracked. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
