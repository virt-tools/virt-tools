Sleep Balance Tracker

Compare recorded sleep with your chosen target and review patterns over time.

Health and safety: this arithmetic is not a medical measurement of “sleep debt,” and sleeping extra does not necessarily undo impairment or health effects. If sleepiness could make driving, operating equipment, or another activity unsafe, stop and choose a safer alternative. Seek qualified medical advice for persistent insomnia, excessive sleepiness, breathing interruptions, or other sleep concerns.

Daily entry

If wake time is earlier than or equal to bed time, wake is treated as the next local calendar day. Otherwise it is same-day sleep.
