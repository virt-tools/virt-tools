ScoreTotal needed

The coefficient is 100 divided by A minus B times e to the power of minus C times body weight, with the A, B and C the IPF published for that combination of sex, equipment and event. It is rounded half up to six decimals, multiplied by the result, and the product is rounded half up to six decimals again, which is the order the IPF document gives and the way its own worked examples land; score sheets print three. 100 points is the reference level of an elite lifter at the same body weight, so the totals in the second table are the results an elite lifter at that weight would need. A result of zero scores zero by the formula. Body weight is taken as measured, in kilograms, and the men's table is defined from 40 kg and the women's from 35 kg. The score compares lifters of different body weights under one federation's rules; it is not a classification, a record standard, a drug-testing result or a prediction of what you will lift. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent, stored or uploaded.
