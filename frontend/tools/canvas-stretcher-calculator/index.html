Canvas Stretcher & Gallery Wrap Calculator

Size a canvas for stretcher bars, or check the largest face a cut canvas can cover.

What are you doing?

The finished face of the stretcher frame, the part that stays visible from the front.

The cut size of a canvas you already have, edge to edge before folding.

Bar depth is the thickness of the stretcher bars, which becomes the visible wrap on each side. Fold-under is how much canvas is pulled around and stapled to the back.

The cut adds the bar depth and fold-under on every side, so a 16 by 20 inch face over 1.5 inch bars with a 2 inch fold-under needs a 23 by 27 inch canvas. For a gallery wrap the image has to run at least the bar depth past the face on each side; the fold-under never shows. The page does not judge whether a bar size suits a canvas or how much extra to add for squaring a stretched edge. Everything is worked out in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
