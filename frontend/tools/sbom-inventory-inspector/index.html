SBOM Inventory Inspector

Explore CycloneDX or SPDX JSON inventories locally, without uploading them.

Inventory aid only: this tool performs structural observations without network requests. It does not query vulnerabilities, verify hashes or signatures, establish provenance/trust, certify license compliance, or prove completeness. License-expression checks are intentionally partial; credential-like URL and purl values are masked in results and exports.
