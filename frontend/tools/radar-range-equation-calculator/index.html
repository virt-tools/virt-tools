The monostatic radar range equation sets the echo at the receiver equal to the minimum detectable signal and solves for range: R4 = P·G2·λ2·σ / ((4π)3·S·L), where G and S are linear, λ = c / f and L is the total system loss as a linear factor. It assumes free-space two-way propagation, one antenna used for both transmit and receive, and a non-fluctuating point target of constant radar cross section. It does not model atmospheric or rain absorption, clutter, the radar horizon, pulse integration, or the probability of detection and false alarm behind the sensitivity figure — check the free-space path loss, receiver sensitivity and link budget tools for those parts.
