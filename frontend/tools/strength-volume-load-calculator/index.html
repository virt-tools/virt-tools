Volume load is the external work counted the standard way: load multiplied by reps gives the volume of one set, further multiplied by the sets in a session and by the sessions in a week. Reps are counted the same way, and relative volume divides the weekly total by bodyweight. Only the weight on the bar or machine is counted — bodyweight exercise, band assistance and the load of the lifter's own body are not modelled — so enter one lift at a time and leave warm-up sets out unless you want them included. The tool is arithmetic on your own entries: it does not judge whether a load, rep range, set count or frequency is appropriate, and it is not coaching or medical advice. Everything runs locally in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
