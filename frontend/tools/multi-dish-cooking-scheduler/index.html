Multi-dish Cooking Scheduler

Back-plan dish steps from a serving time across shared kitchen resources.

Use official recipes and food-safety guidance for temperatures, handling, and hold limits. This scheduler never invents safe temperatures or hold times, does not verify cooking, and is not a food-safety certification.

Plan

Deterministic heuristic: terminal steps target serving time; steps are processed in reverse dependency order by stable ID. Each is placed as late as possible before successors, shifting earlier around existing reservations for its single exclusive resource. Fixed starts are preserved and diagnosed. This is not an optimizer.
