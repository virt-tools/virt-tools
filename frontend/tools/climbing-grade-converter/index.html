Climbing Grade Converter

Convert a rock-climbing grade between the Yosemite Decimal System (5.10a), the French sport scale (6a+), the UIAA scale (VI+) and, for boulder problems, the V-scale (V5) and Fontainebleau (6C+). Type the grade you know; matching rows of the comparison chart are highlighted. Conversions are approximate — published charts differ by up to one step, and gyms often grade softer than rock. Everything runs in your browser.

Discipline

YDS, French or UIAA — for example 5.10a, 6a+ or VI+.

Rows follow a published comparison of the three route scales; they are a guide, not a measurement. Bouldering grades are kept apart from route grades because the two styles are not directly convertible.
