Each ring takes the circumference measured through the middle of the wire, pi times the mandrel plus one wire diameter, plus the kerf the saw turns into dust. Spring-back leaves a coiled ring slightly wider than the mandrel and hand coiling compresses the wire, so cut a short test coil and measure it before committing to a long run. Aspect ratio is the inner diameter divided by the wire diameter; it is the figure chainmail makers quote when comparing rings, and this page does not judge which weaves a ring suits.
