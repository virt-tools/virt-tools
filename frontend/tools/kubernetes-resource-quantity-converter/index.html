    A Kubernetes quantity is a fixed-point number with an optional suffix: m is one thousandth, k to E are powers of ten, and Ki to Ei are powers of 1024. So 250m and 0.25 are the same amount of CPU, and 1.5Gi is 1,610,612,736 bytes. Every figure here is computed exactly in whole units — no floating point — and the total is the sum of the lines as Kubernetes would store them, so a quantity with more than three decimal places is rounded up to the nearest 0.001 first. Nothing is sent or stored.
