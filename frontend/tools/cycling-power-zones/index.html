Cycling Power Zone Calculator

Enter your FTP — or a test effort you can convert to one — and your body mass. The page lays out the seven Coggan power zones in watts and in watts per kilogram, plus the power at a target intensity. Everything runs in your browser.

Enter an FTP, a test power or a body mass to see the zones.

The zones use the seven-level Coggan model as fractions of functional threshold power: active recovery under 55%, endurance 56–75%, tempo 76–90%, lactate threshold 91–105%, VO2 max 106–120%, anaerobic capacity 121–150% and neuromuscular over 150%. Each boundary is the percentage of FTP rounded to the nearest whole watt, and each band runs from one watt above the previous boundary, so no watt belongs to two zones. A 20-minute test is converted at 0.95, an 8-minute test at 0.90 and a ramp-test MAP at 0.75; those factors are population approximations, not measurements of your physiology, and a test only means something if it was paced or ridden all out as the protocol requires. Watts per kilogram divides by the body mass you enter, so it changes nothing about the zones themselves. This is a training-planning aid: it does not set targets for you, diagnose anything, or replace a coach or a medical opinion. Nothing is stored and the page makes no network requests.
