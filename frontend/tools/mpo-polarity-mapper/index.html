The twelve positions through the trunk

Each row is one fibre position at the near plug and the position it reaches at the far plug.

Position map
NearFarUse

A row of 12 positions is the common single-row MPO/MTP array; 5 to 8 stay unused in an 8-fibre application, which transmits on 1 to 4 and receives on 9 to 12. A polarity Method names a whole channel of trunk, adapters, cords and transitions, so parts from two methods are not interchangeable: Method A keeps position 1 on position 1 and needs a Type B cord at one end, Method B reverses the row and uses the same cord type at both ends, Method C crosses each adjacent pair and needs a Type C cord at one end. Multi-row 16- and 24-position connectors, conversion harnesses and cassettes are not modelled here. Pre-terminated assemblies are usually made to order, so check the drawing against your method before ordering and prove the channel with a polarity tester; nothing here replaces that test. Nothing is stored and the page makes no network requests.
