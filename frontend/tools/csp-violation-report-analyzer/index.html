CSP Violation Report Analyzer

Paste CSP violation reports and see which directives actually break, from which origins, and what to change.

A single report, an array, or a Reporting API application/reports+json payload. Both hyphenated and camelCase field names are read.

    Reports are grouped by effective directive and blocked origin, so one noisy resource does not hide the rest. The effective directive is the one that fired; the violated directive is the policy text that named it. Blocked origins keep their scheme and host but drop the path. A disposition of report means the policy was report-only and the resource still loaded. Inline and eval blocks stay as their own origins. Nothing is uploaded, stored or fetched.
