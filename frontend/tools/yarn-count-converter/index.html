Yarn Count Converter

Convert a yarn count between the direct systems (tex, decitex, denier) and the indirect counts (Nm, cotton, worsted, woolen run and linen), including plied yarns.

Direct systems measure mass per length, so a larger number is a thicker yarn; indirect counts measure length per mass, so a larger number is finer. The ply field applies to the count of a single ply: a 2/28 Nm yarn is two plies of 28 Nm and behaves as 14 Nm, which is 71.4 tex. Enter the number after the slash and the ply count, whichever order the label uses. Twisting also adds a little length, so a plied yarn can be marginally finer than the arithmetic result. Values are rounded to three significant figures. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
