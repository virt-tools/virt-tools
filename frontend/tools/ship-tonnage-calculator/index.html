Gross tonnage is GT = K1V with K1 = 0.2 + 0.02 log10V, and net tonnage is NT = K2Vc(4d/3D)² + K3(N1 + N2/10), where K2 = 0.2 + 0.02 log10Vc and K3 = 1.25(GT + 10,000)/10,000. The draft factor is capped at 1, the cargo term is raised to 0.25 GT if it falls below, net tonnage is raised to 0.30 GT if it falls below, and fewer than 13 passengers are counted as zero. The certificate figures are the formula results rounded down to whole numbers, and both tonnages are dimensionless indices, not weights in tonnes. This page does not measure volumes and cannot replace an International Tonnage Certificate (1969) issued by a flag Administration. Everything is calculated in this page; nothing is sent or stored.
