Where the ramp lands

How the ramp is modelled. Playback speed changes in a straight line from the first source frame to the last, so a 4 s clip that starts at 100% and ends at 25% runs for 7.39 s. Because the clip spends longer at the slow end, the average speed is source length ÷ output length, not the mean of the two percentages. The output length is exact for that curve; a frame-based retime rounds each source frame to whole output frames, so the frame count here is the length × frame rate rounded to the nearest frame and can differ by a frame or two from an editor's own count. A clip whose two speeds are equal becomes an ordinary constant retime. Reverse speeds, multi-segment ramps, pitch handling, frame interpolation and render quality settings are outside this page; the frame rate is used only to count frames.
