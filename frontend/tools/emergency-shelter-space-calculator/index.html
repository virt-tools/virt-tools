    What this does not do

    • This is a space-planning estimate, not a building code or licensing determination. Egress widths, fire separation, ventilation, structural capacity, accessibility and local occupancy rules are outside it.
    • The floor is treated as one open, level, evenly usable plate with the chosen non-living share; columns, furniture, fixed equipment, low headroom and circulation between bed spaces are not measured.
    • The people figure is a whole-number count of who fits at the chosen space per person. Real sleeping layouts, household grouping and privacy needs can reduce it.
    • The Sphere figures are humanitarian minimums for covered living space, not legal occupancy limits; settings and cultures may need more, and cold climates use 4.5 m² or more.
    • Reloading the page clears your entries. Nothing is stored, uploaded or sent over the network.
