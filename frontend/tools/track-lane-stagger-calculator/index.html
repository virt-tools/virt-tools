A 400 m oval has two equal straights and two semicircular bends, so the lane 1 measurement radius is (400 − 2 × straight) ÷ 2π. Lanes 2 and out are measured 20 cm from the line on their inside edge, while lane 1 is measured 30 cm from the kerb (or 20 cm from a painted line); the stagger for lane n is therefore 2π × ((n − 1) × lane width + 0.20 m − lane 1 offset). A 200 m race covers one bend, so its stagger is half the 400 m figure, and each lap length is 400 m plus that lane's stagger. Running-surface area counts the two straights plus the ring between the inside edge and the outside of the last lane. This is planning arithmetic only: official track measurement and certification require a survey, and this page does not set competition markings.
