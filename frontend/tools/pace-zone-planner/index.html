Pace Zone Planner

Apply editable percentage bands to a threshold value you enter, with pace and speed shown as exact inverse conversions.

Non-medical, non-prescriptive: This tool performs unit and percentage math only. Preset names and bands are illustrative organizational labels, not individualized training zones, workout advice, safety clearance, or a recommendation to exercise at any intensity. Choose inputs and activity decisions independently, and seek qualified guidance when appropriate.

Math conventions: A 100% boundary equals the entered/derived threshold speed. For a band from L% to H%, speed is threshold × L% through threshold × H%; pace is displayed in the reverse boundary order: distance ÷ (threshold × H%) through distance ÷ (threshold × L%). The example interval column applies the same bounds to the entered distance and is only a conversion example, not a suggested interval or training prescription. Rounding affects display only; exports include higher-precision values.
