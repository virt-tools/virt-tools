Team Capacity Planner

Compare capacity scenarios and inspect every allocation decision without uploading staffing data.

Planning scope: dates are calendar-only and timezone-neutral. Manual assignees are preserved. Automatic allocation is a deterministic greedy heuristic, not an optimal schedule or a promise that work will be completed. Review dependencies, skill depth, interruptions, and human constraints separately.

Horizon and input

1 = Monday through 7 = Sunday. Availability overrides are hours before focus factor; leave dates have zero capacity.

People and work

CSV people columns: id,name,roles,skills,focus,maxParallel,leave,availability. CSV work columns: id,name,role,assignee,effort,start,end,priority. Lists use semicolons; availability uses YYYY-MM-DD:hours pairs. The planning horizon must contain every work item's complete window so deadline results are meaningful.
