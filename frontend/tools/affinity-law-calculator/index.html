For one machine, a speed change n and an impeller trim D scale the duty point as Q ∝ nD, H ∝ n²D² and P ∝ n³D³, so the head factor is the square of the flow factor and the power factor its cube. The laws assume the same impeller geometry, a constant efficiency and a constant fluid density, and they do not intersect the result with the system curve: the real duty point is where the new pump curve crosses the piping curve. A fan's static pressure in pascals is ρgH, with ρ near 1.2 kg/m³ for ambient air. Everything is calculated in this page; nothing is sent or stored.
