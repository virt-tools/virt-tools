Enter current, length, and voltage.

Ampacities are the NEC 75 °C column for copper conductors in free air raceways, with the small-conductor limits (14 AWG → 15 A, 12 AWG → 20 A, 10 AWG → 30 A) applied. Resistance values are copper at 75 °C. The drop calculation assumes a two-conductor DC or single-phase AC round trip; three-phase circuits drop less (√3 factor instead of 2). This estimate ignores ambient-temperature and bundling derating and is not a substitute for your applicable electrical code.
