Every rally scores a point. Regular sets are to 25 and the deciding set to 15, always with a two-point lead and no scoring cap. A team rotates clockwise only when it wins a rally while receiving; position 2 moves to position 1 to serve, and position 1 moves to 6. Change ends when the leading team reaches 8 in a deciding set. P1–P6 are starting-lineup slots, not jersey numbers. Substitutions, libero replacements and multi-set matches are not tracked. Names and scores stay only in this page’s memory.
