Parts per million are milligrams per litre, so the same weight of salt is weaker in a bigger batch. Salts are weighed as the hydrates brewing shops sell: gypsum and calcium chloride dihydrate, Epsom salt heptahydrate. The page reports ion concentrations only — it does not predict mash pH.
