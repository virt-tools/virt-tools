Enter a two-tone measurement to calculate the intercept point.

Both intercepts are extrapolated from two straight lines: the fundamental rises 1 dB per dB of drive and the third-order product 3 dB, so the product sits twice as far below tone 1 as the intercept sits above it. Tone 1 is the carrier that is doubled in 2f1 − f2; swap the tones if you measured 2f2 − f1. The extrapolation assumes a pure third-order slope and ignores gain compression, memory effects and measurement uncertainty. This is an engineering estimate, not a compliance or spectrum-authorisation determination. Everything runs locally in your browser.
