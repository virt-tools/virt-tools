Progression Key Detector

Type a chord progression to see which major or minor key fits it best, ranked by how much of every chord is inside the key.

Chords separated by spaces, commas or bar lines: Am F C G, Dm7 | G7 | Cmaj7. A slash bass is allowed, for example C/E.

Candidate keys

Fit is the weighted share of chord tones inside the key's seven-note scale; ties are broken by where the tonic chord appears.
KeyFitChords in keyOutside the key

Roman numerals

Numerals use the best-fit key, natural minor for minor keys, and mark any chord with tones outside the scale.
#ChordNumeralStatus

The detector compares pitch classes only: inversions, voicings and rhythm are ignored, and a short loop can fit two related keys equally well. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
