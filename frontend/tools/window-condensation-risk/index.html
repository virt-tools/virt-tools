Window Condensation Risk Planner

Compare idealized dew-point margins at window surfaces while keeping uncertainty visible.

Idealized equilibrium only: a surface is flagged when its entered or estimated temperature is at or below the calculated dew point. Actual local humidity, airflow, curtains, thermal bridges, weather, and sensor error vary. This is not a mold or building diagnosis and makes no remediation claim. Inspect real conditions and use qualified help where appropriate.

Room conditions and assumptions

Station pressure is recorded for scenario context; this RH-based Magnus dew-point calculation does not use total pressure. Elevation mode estimates station pressure with a standard atmosphere.

Surface temperatures

Factor mode linearly interpolates between entered outdoor (factor 0) and indoor (factor 1) temperatures; it is not a glazing-performance model. Temperature uncertainty is applied independently to air/dew point and each surface.
