Swatch Internet Time splits the mean solar day into 1,000 equal .beats instead of hours and minutes, so one .beat is 86.4 seconds — 1 minute 26.4 seconds — and an hour is 41⅔ beats. The reading is taken from Biel Mean Time, the fixed UTC+1 offset Swatch chose, which never observes daylight saving; @000 is therefore 23:00 UTC, and the BMT day starts on the previous UTC date. A .beat names a time of day, not a date, so the second panel asks which BMT date to place it on. A watch shows whole beats; this page also reports the exact fraction, counts to the next beat, and shows fractions of a second to 0.1 s. It is the fixed-offset civil scale Swatch defined, not mean solar time measured at Biel, and it ignores leap seconds. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
