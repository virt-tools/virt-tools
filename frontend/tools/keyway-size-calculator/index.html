The key section and both keyway depths come from the DIN 6885-1 table for parallel keys, chosen by the nominal shaft diameter. The bands read “over … to …”, so the top of a band keeps the smaller key: a 30 mm shaft takes the same 8 × 7 mm key as a 25 mm shaft. The depths are measured at the side of the keyway, so the shaft slot measures the diameter minus the shaft depth across the shaft, and the hub slot measures the diameter plus the hub depth through the bore. A normal fit cuts the keyway width N9 in the shaft and JS9 in the hub; P9 is the fixed fit. This is a dimensional reference: it does not size the key for the torque the joint has to carry and it does not check the shaft, hub or key material, so take the strength from the drive design. Everything is computed in this browser: nothing is stored and nothing is sent.
