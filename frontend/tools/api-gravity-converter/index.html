API gravity is defined as 141.5 / SG − 131.5, where SG is the oil's specific gravity at 60 °F relative to water at the same temperature (999.016 kg/m³, the ASTM D1250 value). Density follows as SG × 999.016 kg/m³, and a metric tonne of oil occupies 1,000 / (density × 0.158987294928 m³) barrels, a barrel being 42 US gallons. Grades follow the usual 31.1 and 22.3 °API cut-offs; refiners and the USGS do not all use the same bands, so treat the label as a guide. Values are not temperature-corrected: a reading taken at another temperature must first be brought to 60 °F with the ASTM D1250 tables. Everything runs in this page and nothing is uploaded or stored.
