Multicast MAC Address Mapper

Turn an IPv4 or IPv6 multicast group into the Ethernet address switches forward it as, or work backwards from a multicast MAC to the groups it can belong to.

Address

IPv4 groups run from 224.0.0.0 to 239.255.255.255 and IPv6 groups from ff00::/8. A MAC address may use colons, dashes, Cisco dotted groups or no separators.

      The mapping is defined by RFC 1112 for IPv4 and RFC 2464 for IPv6 and is pure bit arithmetic on the address: nothing is looked up. A switch forwarding table shows only the resulting MAC, so it cannot tell apart the groups that share it, and multicast frames stay inside their Layer 2 domain unless a router is configured to forward them. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
