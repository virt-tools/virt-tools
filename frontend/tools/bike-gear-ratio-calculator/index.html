Gear ratio is chainring ÷ sprocket. Gear inches = diameter × ratio. Development (m) = π × wheel diameter × ratio ÷ 1000. Speed (km/h) = development × cadence × 60 ÷ 1000. Tire diameter is included: effective diameter = bead-seat + 2 × tire width. Runs locally — nothing leaves your browser.
