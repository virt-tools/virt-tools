Set the squad and period details to build a rotation.

Each period fills its open spots with the players who have played the fewest periods so far; a tie goes to whoever has been resting longest, then to the lower player number. That water-filling rule keeps every player within one period of every other, and nobody sits while a fresher player is on the field. Minutes assume whole periods — a player pulled mid-period is not modelled, and positions, match-ups and warm-up order are yours to arrange.
