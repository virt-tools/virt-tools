A bend is measured on the neutral axis, which sits part-way through the thickness: the K-factor says where, as a share of the thickness from the inside face. The legs are outside dimensions, so the flat length is both legs minus one bend deduction.

Developed-length geometry from the values you enter. It does not choose tooling, a minimum bend radius or springback compensation, and it does not check whether the finished part is strong enough for its job.
