Skin depth is the distance at which current density has fallen to 1/e, about 37%, of its surface value: δ = sqrt(2ρ/(ωμ)) = sqrt(ρ/(πfμ0μr)), with ρ = 1/σ. The surface resistance of a smooth conductor is Rs = sqrt(πfμ0μrρ) = 1/(σδ) ohms per square, and about 99% of the current lies within five skin depths. Presets use 20 °C handbook conductivities; edit the conductivity for alloys, plated layers, or another temperature. The model assumes a smooth, thick, homogeneous, non-magnetic conductor in its own metal, so it does not cover thin films, rough foil, layered plating, proximity effect in windings, or magnetic materials whose permeability changes with frequency and field.
