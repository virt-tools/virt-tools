Bounded conversion table

Mass base: g; volume base: mL; count base: each. Supported aliases/conversions:

  • 1 kg = 1,000 g; 1 oz mass = 28.349523125 g; 1 lb = 453.59237 g
  • 1 L = 1,000 mL; 1 US tsp = 4.92892159375 mL; 1 US tbsp = 14.78676478125 mL; 1 US cup = 236.5882365 mL; 1 US fl oz = 29.5735295625 mL
  • each, piece, item, and count share count dimension. Unknown units combine only with the exact same normalized unit.

Mass and volume never cross-convert because no ingredient density is assumed.
