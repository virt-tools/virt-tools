A conversion is taken on the line through the point where the try was grounded, parallel to the touchlines, and the goal posts stand 5.6 m apart on the goal line with the crossbar 3 m up. Put the kicker at x metres from the posts' centre line and y metres behind it: the two uprights sit at (±2.8, 0), so the angle between them is atan2(5.6y, x² + y² − 2.8²). Inside the posts that angle simply falls as you move back, so the closest stance gives the widest view. Outside them it rises to a peak and falls again: differentiating gives y² = x² − 2.8², so a try 20 m off centre is widest from about 19.8 m back. The table marks that spot when it falls inside the 60 m the page models. The angle is geometry only — it says nothing about wind, distance, the kicker's range or the height of the crossbar, and a wider angle from further out is not automatically an easier kick. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
