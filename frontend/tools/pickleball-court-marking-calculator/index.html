Net height at the center
34 in (86.36 cm), plus or minus 0.25 in, at the center point of the court; the strap is part of the measurement.
Post spacing
22 ft (6.71 m) plus or minus 1 in between the inside faces, which puts each post about 1 ft outside its sideline on a 20 ft court. Posts are at most 3 in in diameter.
Net size
At least 21 ft 9 in (6.63 m) from post to post and 30 in (76.2 cm) deep, with a 2 in white tape along the top.

Every measurement follows USA Pickleball's court specification: the court is a 20 by 44 ft rectangle measured to the outside edge of the perimeter and non-volley zone lines, the zone is 7 ft deep on each side of the net, and the centerline runs from the zone line to the baseline, so the service court is 10 by 15 ft. A regulation court needs a playing surface of at least 30 by 60 ft, which is 5 ft beyond each sideline and 8 ft behind each baseline; the page keeps that margin around whatever size you enter. Paint area assumes 2 in lines and counts each crossing once. This is marking arithmetic for a flat surface: it does not survey a site, square a slab, or replace the rules of the competition you play in. Everything is calculated in this page and nothing is stored or sent.
