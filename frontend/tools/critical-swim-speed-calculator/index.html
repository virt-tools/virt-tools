Critical Swim Speed Calculator

Enter the times from a 400 and a 200 time trial to get your critical swim speed, the equivalent threshold pace per 100 metres or yards, the distance reserve behind the 400, and the pace and repetition time at each percentage of CSS speed. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is uploaded, stored or sent anywhere.

    What this does not do

    • Critical swim speed is a model of the sustainable rate taken from two maximal trials. Swimmers usually hold it for tens of minutes rather than indefinitely, so treat it as a threshold anchor, not a cruising pace.
    • Pacing errors in either trial move the result a lot, and a 25 m and a 50 m pool give different values because of turns.
    • It is a training aid, not a lab measurement or medical advice, and it says nothing about technique, injury or readiness to train.
    • Nothing is saved: reloading the page clears your entries.
