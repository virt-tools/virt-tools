Paper Weight Converter

Convert a paper weight between grams per square metre and the US basis weights for bond, text, cover, index, bristol and tag, using each grade’s own base sheet.

A US basis weight is the weight of a 500-sheet ream of that grade’s standard base sheet, so one metric figure becomes a different pound figure for every grade: 80 g/m² is about 21 lb bond, 54 lb text or 30 lb cover. Caliper is not implied, because thickness also depends on the paper’s bulk. Values are rounded to one decimal place. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
