Hebrew Calendar Converter

Turn a Gregorian date into its Hebrew date — or a Hebrew date back into the civil calendar — with the year type, month length and weekday.

Direction

Hebrew months run from Tishrei to Elul; a leap year adds Adar I before Adar II. Years 1600 to 2200 are supported.

The Hebrew calendar here is the fixed arithmetic calendar: a 19-year cycle with seven leap years, month lengths set by the year length (353, 354 or 355 days in a common year and 383, 384 or 385 in a leap year), and Rosh Hashanah postponed so it never falls on a Sunday, Wednesday or Friday. That is the calendar in religious use, not an observational one, so the date does not depend on when the new moon was sighted. The result is the Hebrew date in force during the daylight hours of the civil date you enter; a Hebrew day begins at sunset, so the evening before the same civil date already belongs to the next Hebrew date. Adar I appears only in leap years. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
