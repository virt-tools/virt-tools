Zoom is measured against the frame after the source is scaled to cover it, so 100% shows the whole frame and 200% shows a quarter of its area. Pan positions are fractions of the room left over once the crop is placed. Zoom and pan move linearly from start to end; apply easing in the editor if you want a softer start.
