Came runs along every seam of the panel. Each inside edge is shared by two pieces, so the came length is half the sum of all the piece perimeters plus the panel perimeter itself: (sum of perimeters + panel perimeter) ÷ 2. Copper foil is a strip around every edge of every piece, so its length is the sum of the piece perimeters, plus a little overlap where the ends meet. Solder runs along both faces of every seam, so its length is the sum of the piece perimeters plus the panel perimeter. Glass area is width × height for each piece, added up; the panel area is shown beside it and a coverage above 100% means the pieces are larger than the panel you typed. The estimate assumes the pieces tile the panel edge to edge, with no gaps or overlaps, and it is a buying figure only: it does not allow for cutting waste, so order a little more glass than this, and note that bevels, textured glass and the heart width of the came all change the size each piece is cut to, so cut from your pattern rather than from these totals.
