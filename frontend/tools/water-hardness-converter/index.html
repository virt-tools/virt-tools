Water Hardness Converter

Convert a hardness figure between mg/L as calcium carbonate, grains per US gallon, the three European degree scales and millimoles per litre, and see the USGS hardness class it falls in.

Every scale here expresses the same thing: the calcium and magnesium in the water counted as calcium carbonate. One German degree is 10 mg CaO per litre, or 17.848 mg/L as CaCO3; one English or Clark degree is one grain per imperial gallon, 14.254 mg/L; one French degree is 10 mg/L; and one grain per US gallon is 17.118 mg/L, which is why the US figure is larger than the imperial one. Millimoles per litre count formula units of CaCO3, so 1 mmol/L is 100.09 mg/L. The USGS class is a general guideline for drinking water, not a treatment recommendation: a softener’s own capacity rating and your local water report decide the setting. Everything is calculated in your browser; nothing is sent or stored.
