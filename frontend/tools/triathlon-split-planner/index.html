How the target is split up

The swim and run legs use the paces you type, the bike leg takes whatever is left of the target after both legs and both transitions, and its average speed is the distance over that time. Pick a different leg under Solve for to hold the bike speed and let the target set the swim or run pace instead. Every figure is an average for the whole leg: no wind, hills, drafting, wetsuit or sighting are modelled, and the transitions are treated as fixed times you entered. Distances are metric; enter 21.0975 for a half marathon. Everything is computed locally in your browser; nothing is stored or sent.
