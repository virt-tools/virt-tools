How hands rank

    Standard ace-high ranking, with the ace also playing low in A-2-3-4-5. With six or seven cards every five-card combination is tried and the strongest is kept, so the result lists the exact cards that play. One standard 52-card deck only: no wild cards, jokers, short-deck variants, odds or betting advice.
