Printable Paper Generator

Make your own stationery: ruled lines, a dot grid, graph paper or a Cornell notes sheet, sized for standard paper. Sheets are drawn in millimetres and print at true scale — everything runs locally in your browser.

between lines or dots
1 to 12 identical sheets
Choose a ruling and spacing, then generate.

Each sheet is a vector drawing with the page size set to the paper you choose, so printing at 100% keeps the ruling at its true spacing. In the print dialog pick Actual size (100%) and No margins; use “Save as PDF” for a digital copy. Printers cannot always reach the sheet edge, so keep at least 10 mm of margin if the border matters. Rule spacing is measured between adjacent lines or dot centres; the Cornell layout uses a cue column 30% of the text width and a summary band across the bottom quarter. Nothing is stored, uploaded or sent.
