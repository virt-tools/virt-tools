Football Passer Rating Calculator

Turn a passing stat line into an NFL passer rating and see the four capped components that produce it.

passes thrown, excluding sacks
passes caught by the offence
net yards gained on those passes
scoring passes caught in the end zone
passes caught by the defence
Enter the passing totals to calculate the rating.

Uses the NFL passer rating, whose four components each count 0 to 2.375 points: completion percentage, yards per attempt, touchdown percentage and interception percentage. The rating is the sum divided by six and multiplied by 100, so 158.3 is perfect and zeros are possible. Clipping at the component limits means very strong or very weak games cannot push the score past those bounds. It is a single-game or season summary statistic, not a prediction, and it makes no allowance for sacks, drops, opponent strength, era, weather or rushing value. Everything runs in your browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
