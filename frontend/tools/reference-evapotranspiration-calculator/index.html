How the figure is worked out. The page applies the daily FAO-56 Penman-Monteith equation for the hypothetical grass reference surface: 0.12 m tall, surface resistance 70 s m⁻¹ and albedo 0.23, with the soil heat flux taken as zero over 24 hours. Saturation vapour pressure comes from the maximum and minimum temperatures, actual vapour pressure from the two humidity readings, and net radiation from sunshine through the Ångström relation with as = 0.25 and bs = 0.50. The equation is ET₀ = [0.408 Δ (Rn − G) + γ (900/(T + 273)) u₂ (es − ea)] / [Δ + γ (1 + 0.34 u₂)]. Wind measured above 2 m is converted to the standard height. This is reference grass, not a particular crop: crop water use is Kc × ET₀ with the crop and growth-stage coefficient, and a watering decision also needs rainfall, soil water storage and rooting depth, which this page does not model. The equations are usually applied up to about 66° latitude; polar day and night are outside the model. Sunshine is the only radiation input, so a site with measured solar radiation may differ. Everything is calculated in the browser with no storage, uploads or network access.
