Enter the pins knocked down in each frame to see the frame scores and running total.

Enter 10 for a strike and leave the next box empty. An open frame scores its two balls; a spare scores 10 plus your next ball; a strike scores 10 plus your next two balls. Frame 10 gives bonus balls after a strike or spare. Handicap, fouls, no-tap scoring and split conversion are not included — this is scratch ten-pin scoring.
