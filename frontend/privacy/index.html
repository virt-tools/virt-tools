Privacy & Trust

No accounts, no analytics, no ads.

On your device

Tool inputs stay local: calculations run in this tab and results are never uploaded. The site stores your theme, sort order, saved tools and feedback IDs in this browser. Search text is kept for the session so Back works.

Feedback

The feedback form sends the kind, tool and message you enter. Don’t include passwords or personal records. You get a UUID to check the reply; entries are deleted after 365 days by default. Open feedback.

Offline cache

The app may cache pages you visit so they open offline. API responses and feedback are never cached.
